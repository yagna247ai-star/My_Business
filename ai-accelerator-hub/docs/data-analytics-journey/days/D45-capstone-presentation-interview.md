# Day 45 — Telling Your Story to an Owner and an Interviewer

> **Time:** 4 h 30 min · **Module:** M9 Capstone · **Shop:** Your chosen capstone

## The problem
Your project is finished. Now you must explain it to two people:
- A shop owner, who does not know any technical words.
- A technical reviewer, who will ask "why did you do that?"

Then you must answer interview questions for a junior analyst job.

## The big idea
A good five-minute talk follows the owner's story:
1. **The question**: what the owner wanted to know.
2. **What you did**: in plain words.
3. **What you found**: one main finding, with a number.
4. **How sure you are**: one limit of the data.
5. **What to do next**: one action the owner can take.

In an interview, use the **STAR** method for "tell me about a time" questions:
- **S**ituation: what was happening?
- **T**ask: what did you need to do?
- **A**ction: what did you do?
- **R**esult: what happened?

## Words to know
- **Viva**: a spoken test where a reviewer asks about your work.
- **STAR**: Situation, Task, Action, Result.
- **Limit**: a fact that makes your answer less certain.

## Let's solve it
Two openings for the same project:
- **Weak**: "I used SQL, Python and Power BI to analyse sales data."
- **Strong**: "Sunita's owner asked which products drive revenue in each branch. Three products make up 40% of October's revenue in North. The strongest branch is not the one with the most orders. The data covers one season only, so I would not change stock on this alone."

The strong opening answers the owner's question, gives a number, and names a limit.

## Your turn
1. Prepare a five-minute talk. Use the five steps above, one slide each.
2. Answer five viva questions about your project, from your trainer or a friend.
3. Do a short timed test: one SQL query with a join, one cleaning task in Python, and one question about which chart to use.
4. Do three practice interviews. Record each one. Use STAR for the "tell me about a time" questions. Use these:
   - Walk me through a dataset you cleaned. What did you change?
   - What is the difference between LEFT JOIN and INNER JOIN?
   - How would you find the top product in each branch?
   - Which chart would you use to show a trend over time, and why?
   - How did you check your numbers?
   - Tell me about a time an AI tool gave you a wrong answer.

**Hand in:** your talk (slides or a recording), your viva notes, your test score, and the three practice interviews with feedback.

## Check yourself (trainer's answer key)
1. The owner needs your answer and one action. The reviewer needs your method.
2. Each answer to a viva question gives a reason for your choice.
3. Say which skills are beginner-level. Honesty about your level is a strength.

**Expected:** Your talk covers all five steps and names one limit. Each interview answer uses a number or an example from your project.

## Why this helps your job
Your talk and your interview answers are what get you the job. Practise them out loud.
