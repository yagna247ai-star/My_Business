## Objective
Complete one end-to-end analytics project for a real-world-style MSME problem, present it to a business owner and a technical reviewer, and prepare for entry-level interviews.

## Learning outcomes
- Turn a vague owner request into a clear, testable business question.
- Extract, query, clean and transform the project data, and keep a log of each step.
- Analyse the data with SQL and Python, and build a Power BI dashboard that answers the question.
- Use AI tools for insight drafting, and verify each claim.
- Present the work in five minutes, answer a viva, and explain every choice.

## Job readiness pack
Every learner leaves M9 with:
1. A **portfolio** of their deliverables from Days 6–44.
2. A **one-page case study** of the capstone (problem, method, result, limit).
3. A **mock-interview log** from Day 45 (questions asked, answers given, feedback).
4. A **skills sheet** that lists only skills shown in the portfolio. It makes no claim about ML or production ETL beyond what was built.
