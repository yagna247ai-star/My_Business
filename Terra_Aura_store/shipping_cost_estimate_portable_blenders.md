## Battery Shipping Requirements (CRITICAL)

Portable blenders with built-in 6000mAh Li-ion batteries are classified as:

> **UN3481 — Lithium-ion batteries CONTAINED IN equipment**
> IATA Packing Instruction: **PI967** (Section I or II)

### Required Documentation
1. **UN38.3 Test Report** — mandatory for every battery model exported from China. Must be from accredited lab (SGS, UL, TÜV, CMA). Cost: ~RMB 6,000–15,000 per model, lead time 4–6 weeks. Validity typically 1–3 years.
2. **SDS/MSDS** (Safety Data Sheet) — 16-section GHS-compliant document authored by the manufacturer.
3. **DG Packaging Certificate** — required for ocean freight from China (issued by customs inspection authority).
4. **DGD (Dangerous Goods Declaration)** — required for air freight (IATA Shipper's Declaration) and ocean (IMO DGD).

### Mode-Specific Rules
- **Air (PI967 Section II):** Permitted on passenger AND cargo aircraft if ≤4 cells / ≤2 batteries per package. No mandatory SOC limit. Must have lithium battery handling mark. DG surcharge applies but less than standalone batteries.
- **Air (PI967 Section I):** Full DG declaration required. Higher documentation burden.
- **Ocean (IMDG Class 9):** Requires DG-licensed CFS warehouse for LCL. For FCL, requires DG-compliant container packing certificate. Carriers (MSC, Maersk, CMA CGM) often require 7+ days advance DG review.
- **Express Couriers (DHL/FedEx/UPS):** Each has internal DG policies. Generally accept UN3481 PI967 but may require pre-approval and UN38.3 on file.

## Duties & Taxes (NOT included in shipping estimates above)

### United States
- **HS Code:** 8509.40.00.15 (Blenders)
- **MFN Base Rate:** 4.2%
- **Section 301 (China):** 10% IEEPA tariff (reduced from 20%) + potential 12.5% Forced Labor tariff (announced July 23, 2026) = total ~26.7% on some goods
- **Effective rate from China (estimated, August 2026):** **~15–30%** of CIF value depending on exact product classification and whether forced labor tariff applies
- **De minimis:** The $800 de minimis exemption was **eliminated in February 2026** for Chinese-origin goods — ALL shipments now incur duties regardless of value
- **Sources:** USTR Federal Register July 23, 2026; tariffs.wove.com (HS 8509.40 from China: 16.7%); gingercontrol.com (effective ~33.9% as of Jan 2026)

### India
- **HS Code:** 8509.40 (Food grinders/mixers; kitchen appliances)
- **Basic Customs Duty (BCD):** ~10–20% (electronics/kitchen appliances category)
- **Social Welfare Surcharge (SWS):** 10% of BCD
- **IGST:** 18% on (CIF value + BCD + SWS)
- **Effective total duty burden:** **~29–42%** of CIF value
- **Example:** On CIF value of $100: BCD $10–$20 → SWS $1–$2 → IGST 18% of ~$111–$122 = ~$20–$22 → total duty ~$31–$44
- **Express courier shipments:** India assesses duties on all commercial imports; no de minimis for business shipments
- **Sources:** india-briefing.com; eximpe-blog; chinacartbridge.com (15–40% total); wise.com import duty calculator

## Key Takeaways

1. **For samples (1 unit):** Use FedEx or DHL express. Expect $40–$60 to US, $30–$45 to India. Budget 3–8 days transit.

2. **For 10–50 units:** Air freight (consolidated service via a freight forwarder) is cheaper than LCL sea freight because LCL minimum charges dominate at low volumes. Surprising but true: air wins on both cost and speed up to ~50 units.

3. **Sea freight LCL becomes viable at ~100–200+ units** when volumes reach ~1 CBM or more. Below this, minimum charges make it uneconomical.

4. **FCL sea freight is the clear winner at 500+ units** (about 1/4 pallet or more). India FCL rates are remarkably low right now ($1,395–$1,705 for a 20ft container — August 2026 rates).

5. **Battery compliance is non-negotiable.** Budget RMB 6,000–15,000 (~$800–$2,000) upfront for UN38.3 testing per battery model. Without this, no carrier will accept your shipment.

6. **Duties are additional.** Factor 15–30% on CIF for US imports, and 29–42% for India imports, on top of shipping costs.

7. **All rates are indicative.** Freight rates are volatile. The figures above are August 2026 snapshot estimates. Always obtain live quotes from 2–3 freight forwarders before booking. Rates can shift 10–20% month to month.
