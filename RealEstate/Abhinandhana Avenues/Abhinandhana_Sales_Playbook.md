# Abhinandhana Avenues Pvt Ltd — GM Sales Playbook
## Fixing Lead-to-Conversion for Open Plots with EMI Options (AP + Telangana)

**Prepared for**: GM Sales
**Audience**: GM Sales, Telecallers, Sales Executives, Field Agents
**Core problem**: High lead volume, low close rate. Leads are coming — the funnel is leaking.
**Core thesis**: You don't have a lead problem. You have a **speed-to-lead problem, a follow-up problem, and a site-visit problem**. All three are fixable with process, not more spend.

---

## 1. THE FUNNEL — Where the Leak Is

### 1.1 Funnel Stages, Benchmarks, and Leak Points

| Stage | What Happens | Industry Benchmark | Abhinandhana Likely Now | Primary Fix |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|------------------------|-------------|
| 1. Lead Capture | Enquiry via call, WhatsApp, Meta lead form, walk-in, referral | — | High volume (your strength) | Maintain. Add lead source tracking |
| 2. First Response | First contact within SLA | **Within 5 min = 21x conversion** vs 30 min | 🔴 **Leak #1: slow response** | WhatsApp auto-reply + call-back SLA |
| 3. Qualification | Budget, location, EMI capacity, timeline, decision maker | 60–70% qualify | ~50% (no scripted qualification) | Qualification script (Section 3) |
| 4. Site Visit Booked | Appointment scheduled from qualified lead | 25–30% of qualified | ~15% | Follow-up cadence (Section 2) |
| 5. Visit Attended | Lead actually shows up | 65–70% show rate | 🔴 **Leak #2: 50% no-show** | Confirmation protocol (Section 4) |
| 6. Offer Made | Sales exec presents price + EMI plan | 40–50% of visitors | ~35% | Offer discipline, never let them leave without an offer |
| 7. Token / Booking | Token amount paid | 25–30% of offers | ~15% | Urgency levers + token strategy (Section 5) — target 35–40% |
| 8. Registration / Payment | Agreement signed, EMI plan executed | 85–90% of bookings | ~70% (EMI drop-offs) | Paper process hand-holding |

**End-to-end benchmark (current state, leaks unfixed)**: 100 cold leads → 60 qualified → 15–18 visits booked → 10–12 attended → 4–6 offers → 1–2 bookings. **Overall conversion: 1–2%** — this is what "high volume, low close rate" actually looks like.

**After 90 days of fixes (target state)**: 100 leads → 65 qualified → 20–24 booked → 14–17 attended → 7–9 offers → 3–4 bookings. **Overall conversion: 3–5%.** The 90-day plan in Section 6 is built to move the funnel from the first line to the second.

### 1.2 The Leak Point to Fix FIRST

**Fix order (by ROI)**:
1. **First-response SLA (Leak #1)** — cheapest to fix, biggest multiplier. A lead contacted in 5 minutes converts up to 21x better than one contacted in 30 minutes. If Abhinandhana currently responds in hours or next-day, this single change can double conversions within 2 weeks.
2. **Site-visit show rate (Leak #2)** — you're paying for visits that never happen. Confirmation protocol alone typically lifts show rate from 50% to 70%.
3. **Offer-to-booking (Leak #3)** — visitors who like the plot but leave "to think." Fix with same-visit offer structure + 48-hour token window.

**Do NOT touch lead generation until 1–3 are fixed.** More leads into a leaking funnel = more wasted money and demoralized telecallers.

## 2. LEAD RESPONSE + FOLLOW-UP SYSTEM

### 2.1 Response SLA (Non-Negotiable)

| Channel | SLA | Owner | Tool |
|---------|-----|-------|------|
| WhatsApp enquiry | **< 1 minute** (auto) + human reply < 5 min | Telecaller (WhatsApp Business auto-reply + quick replies) | WhatsApp Business |
| Meta lead form / Ad enquiry | **< 5 minutes** | Telecaller on shift | Meta Instant Forms + WhatsApp click-to-chat; auto-notification via ManyChat free tier |
| Phone call (missed) | Call back **< 10 minutes** | Telecaller | Missed-call log in CRM |
| Walk-in / referral | Same day, < 2 hours | Sales exec | CRM task |

**Roster rule**: During ad hours (9am–9pm, especially weekends), a telecaller must be "on-response duty" — no dialing out, only answering incoming. Rotate every 2 hours. One missed lead because everyone was dialing = pure waste.

### 2.2 The 90-Day Follow-Up Cadence

Every qualified lead goes into a 90-day nurturing track, mapped to funnel stage:

| Day | Touchpoint | Channel | Goal | Content to Send |
|-----|-----------|---------|------|-----------------|
| **Day 0 (5 min)** | First response | WhatsApp + Call | Warm + qualify basic info | Greeting, 1 project link, 2 quick questions (budget, location) |
| Day 1 | Follow-up | Call | Confirm interest, book visit | Project brochure PDF + price list |
| Day 2 | Value add | WhatsApp | Build trust | 2–3 site photos / drone video, one customer testimonial |
| Day 3 | Visit push | Call | **Book site visit** | Weekend slot offer + "limited plots in your budget" |
| Day 7 | Nudge | WhatsApp | Confirm/rebook | RERA registration number + approval docs screenshot |
| Day 14 | Value add | WhatsApp | Stay warm (if no visit) | EMI calculator screenshot for their budget band |
| Day 21 | Visit push 2 | Call | Rebook visit | New offer: free site visit + guidance session |
| Day 30 | Value add | WhatsApp | Trust building | Quarterly phase progress photo / price increase alert |
| Day 45 | Check-in | Call | Re-qualify (still interested? budget changed?) | Fresh price list + remaining inventory |
| Day 60 | Social proof | WhatsApp | Overcome hesitation | 2 video testimonials from same-city buyers |
| Day 75 | Final push | Call | Closing attempt | "Last 5 plots in your band" + token offer |
| Day 90 | Exit or recycle | Call + WhatsApp | Close or move to database | Final offer; if no → tag "cold, recycle after 6 months" |

**The rule**: No lead is "dead" in under 90 days. In Indian real estate, the average plotted-development sale takes 45–90 days from first contact. Most competitors give up at 30 days — that's your edge.

### 2.4 Lead Source Tagging (Do This From Day 1)

## 3. EMI SELLING FRAMEWORK

### 3.1 The Simple EMI Math (Your Salespeople Must Memorize This)

Present EMI as **"monthly coffee money"**, never as total price. Rule: **never quote the full price first. Quote the daily cost.**

**Daily-cost framing** (works universally):
> "Sir, this plot costs ₹8 lakh. But with our EMI plan, it's just **₹11,000 per month** — that's **₹366 per day**. Less than what you spend on chai and snacks. And 30 months later, you own a piece of land that's appreciating while you sleep."

**EMI Bands — Qualify by Monthly Capacity, Not Total Budget**

| Monthly Capacity | Loan/EMI Term Suggestion | Plot Value Band | What to Show Them |
|------------------|--------------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| ₹5,000/month | 30–48 months | ₹4–7 lakh | Small plots / early-phase plots (best rate per sq.yd) |
| ₹10,000/month | 36–60 months | ₹8–15 lakh | Mid-size plots (150–200 sq.yd) |
| ₹15,000/month | 36–60 months | ₹12–22 lakh | Premium positioning plots (corner, road-facing) |
| ₹25,000/month | 36–60 months | ₹20–35 lakh | Best-in-phase plots, larger plots (250+ sq.yd) |

**Qualification question (ask on first call, not later)**:
> "Sir, thinking practically — how much can you comfortably pay every month for this plot, without stretching? ₹5,000, ₹10,000, or more? I'll show you exactly the plot and plan that fits that."

**Why bands work**: Asking for a band (not a precise number) is non-threatening — Telugu buyers hesitate to state exact budgets. The band instantly tells the salesperson what to show, killing the "show everything, book nothing" problem.

### 3.2 "Too Expensive" Objection — Script

> **Lead**: "చాలా ఖరీదుగా ఉంది రా" / "It's too expensive."
>
> **Salesperson**: "Sir, I understand completely. Before you decide, let me ask — are you comparing this with another plot, or with your monthly savings plan? Because there's a difference. [pause] If it's the monthly payment — ₹11,000 a month, that's about what a family spends on weekend outings. And unlike outings, this money comes back — with appreciation. Last year this same phase was ₹X/sq.yd. Today it's ₹X+15%. Next year, this plot will look 15% cheaper. The question isn't whether you can afford it — it's whether you can afford to wait."
>
> **Telugu**: "సార్, నేను పూర్తిగా అర్థం చేసుకున్నాను. కానీ ఒక విషయం చెప్పండి — మీరు వేరే ప్లాట్తో పోల్చుతున్నారా, లేక మీ నెలవారీ పొదుపుతో పోల్చుతున్నారా? తేడా ఉంది. నెలకు ₹11,000 — ఒక కుటుంబం వీకెండ్ ట్రిప్పులకు ఖర్చు చేసేంత. కానీ ఈ డబ్బు తిరిగి వస్తుంది — అప్రిసియేషన్తో. గత సంవత్సరం ఇదే ఫేజ్ ₹X/sq.yd. ఇప్పుడు ₹X+15%. వచ్చే ఏడాది ఈ ప్లాట్ 15% చౌకగా కనిపిస్తుంది. ప్రశ్న మీరు కొనగలరా అని కాదు — వేచి ఉండగలరా అని."

**The 3-part structure**: Validate → Reframe (comparison, not price) → Future-price urgency. Never argue about the price itself — you lose that fight. Reframe to time.

### 3.3 EMI Objection — "I'll pay full later / No EMI needed now"

> "Great — if you're paying full, you qualify for our best phase price and an extra discount of ₹X. But here's my advice, sir: this phase is being registered right now. You pay the token today, lock today's rate, and choose the payment mode later. If you wait, the next phase is automatically 10–15% higher. Why pay more for the same land?"

**Golden rule**: EMI is the *entry ticket*, not the only path. Always present token-first, payment-mode-second.

## 4. SITE VISIT PLAYBOOK

### 4.1 Pre-Visit Protocol (Lifts Show Rate from 50% to 70%)

| When | Action | Tool |
|------|--------|------|
| On booking | Confirm date + time + **number of family members coming** + exact pickup point | CRM task |
| 24 hours before | WhatsApp: "Tomorrow 10am at [pickup]. Here's the location pin and my number. Confirm?" — require a "yes" reply | WhatsApp quick reply |
| 2 hours before | Call: "On the way? Should I keep the car waiting?" | Call |
| **Show-rate rule** | If no confirmation reply at 24h → **rebook immediately** with another slot. Never let an unconfirmed visit go to waste | — |

**The single most powerful question at booking**:
> "Sir, who will be coming with you — just you, or family? **Please bring the family — especially whoever will be part of the decision.** The land should be seen by everyone who decides."

This sentence alone converts "single-visit, then family says no" into "one visit, whole family, decision on the spot."

### 4.2 The Visit Agenda (60–75 Minutes, Structured)

| Time | Segment | What Happens | Who |
|------|---------|--------------|-----|
| 0–5 min | Reception | Welcome, water/tea, small talk. No selling yet. Build warmth. | Sales exec |
| 5–15 min | Layout walk | Walk the master layout. Show **their budget band's plots first**. Let them walk the actual plot. Let them feel the soil, measure with a tape. | Sales exec |
| 15–25 min | Amenities & infrastructure | Show roads, drainage, park area, boundary, gates. Point to real physical things. | Sales exec + Site manager |
| 25–35 min | Future development plan | Show the phase map — what's built, what's coming. **Show the price increase history of previous phases** (this is your strongest trust tool). | Site manager |
| 35–45 min | RERA & approvals | Show RERA registration number, DTCP approvals, title documents. Say the words: "This is fully RERA-registered. All approvals are in place. Here's the document." | Sales exec |
| 45–55 min | Investment math + EMI | Sit down (shade/office). Whiteboard or tablet: today's price, appreciation math, EMI plan for their band. | Sales exec |
| 55–65 min | Offer + close attempt | Formal offer, token amount, next steps. **Never end without an offer on the table.** | Sales exec |
| 65–75 min | If not closing | Book the decision date. "When can I expect your answer? Let's fix a call for Tuesday evening." | Sales exec |

### 4.3 The Closing Window

- **Best window**: On-site, right after the offer. Fresh excitement + full family present = peak decision readiness.
- **Second window**: 48 hours post-visit. Token held at today's rate for 48 hours. After that, price protection lapses (genuine or framed — either way, it's a discipline).
- **Death zone**: "I'll think and call you." Without a fixed callback date, this is a lost lead. Always convert to a specific date.

### 4.4 Common Site-Visit Mistakes (Audit Your Team Against This)

1. **Showing plots outside the budget band** — creates confusion and kills trust. Show the best plot in their band, not all plots.
2. **No family present** — the biggest single cause of "I need to discuss with family." Prevent it at booking.
3. **Documents not ready at visit** — RERA copy, approvals, layout map must be printed and in hand. Disorganized docs = "something to hide" to buyers.
4. **No offer made** — salespeople avoid the ask. The visit is a failure without an offer on the table.
5. **Sales exec alone** — the site manager should attend the future-plan and approvals segment. Authority present = faster decisions.
6. **Letting them leave without next step** — every visitor leaves with either a token, or a fixed decision date, or a rebooked visit. Nothing else.

## 5. CLOSING TECHNIQUES (Indian Real Estate Context)

### 5.1 Urgency Levers (Ranked by Credibility — Use Genuine Ones)

| Lever | How It Works | Script Opening | Credibility |
|-------|-------------|----------------|-------------|
| **Phase price increase** | "Next phase launches 10–15% higher; this phase's rate holds till [date]" | "The new phase price list is already printed. This phase's rates are valid till [date]." | ⭐⭐⭐ Highest — buyers see previous phases' history |
| **RERA milestone** | Registration of this phase is in progress; rates re-price post-registration | "Once this phase registers, prices officially re-rate. Today's price is pre-registration." | ⭐⭐⭐ Very high — documentable |
| **Limited inventory in band** | "Only N plots left in the ₹8–12 lakh band" — must be TRUE | "I checked the inventory list this morning — 3 plots left in your band." | ⭐⭐ High — verify it's true |
| **Registration deadline** | Agreement + registration window closes [date] | "If we register this month, your stamp duty locks at current rates." | ⭐⭐ Good for closing the paper process |
| **Festival/launch scheme** | Genuine limited-period offer | "The [festival] launch offer ends this Sunday." | ⭐ Medium — overused; use rarely |

**Integrity rule**: Never fake inventory or deadlines. A buyer who catches one lie tells 10 people. In a market driven by referrals and family networks (AP/TS especially), one fake urgency = one dead referral chain.

### 5.2 Token Amount Strategy

| Step | Amount | Purpose |
|------|--------|---------|
| On-site token (day of visit) | ₹25,000–50,000 | Lock the plot + price. Low enough to say yes, high enough to be real commitment |
| Booking amount (within 7 days) | ₹50,000–1,00,000 (or 5–10% of plot value) | Formal booking, starts the EMI/agreement process |
| Registration | Per RERA/agreement terms | Final step |

**Token scripts**:
> "Sir, to lock this exact plot at today's price, all we need is ₹25,000 today. It's fully adjustable into the booking amount. If for any genuine reason you change your mind within 7 days, we return it [per your policy — say this ONLY if true]. The only thing it does is make sure this plot isn't sold to someone else while you're deciding. Shall I prepare the receipt?"

**Rules**:
- Always give a receipt. Always state the refund terms in writing. (RERA + consumer protection are real in AP/TS now.)
- Token converts "I'll think" into a concrete commitment — and committed buyers rarely walk.
- The 7-day adjustment window keeps the token low-pressure. Most tokens convert to full bookings.

### 5.3 Family-Decision Handling

**The reality**: In Telugu/AP/TS homebuying, the decision rarely rests with the single lead. It rests with a family unit — often the father, sometimes the elder brother, occasionally the wife.

| Situation | Tactic |
|-----------|--------|
| Lead says "I need to discuss with family" | "Of course — who will be part of the decision? [Get names]. Let me arrange a **second site visit with everyone present** — I'll keep the car ready. When is everyone free — Saturday or Sunday?" — Turn discussion into a group visit, never a solo "thinking period." |
| Family member raises objection at visit | Sales exec must have the **family-oriented objection scripts**: "This is for the children's future" / "Land is the safest investment in India — it can't run away" / "Property prices in this corridor grew X% last year" |
| Elder says "prices will come down" | "Ji, land never goes down in this corridor — it goes sideways at worst. And with EMI, you're paying today's price over time. If prices go up, you win. If they stay flat, you still own land at today's rate. There's no scenario where the buyer loses." |
| Decision deferred 3+ times | Apply a light-touch urgency: "The plots in your band are now down to [N]. I don't want you to come back and see it sold." + offer to hold till a specific date. |

**Golden rule**: Never negotiate against a family member. Always add value to the family unit: "This isn't just a plot — it's a future house for your son/daughter, a retirement asset, a wedding fund that grows."

## 9. THE 5 RULES TO PIN ON THE WALL

1. **5 minutes or lose.** Every lead answered in 5 minutes. Speed beats everything.
2. **Qualify by monthly band, not by "budget."** ₹5k/10k/15k/25k — know their band before you show a single plot.
3. **No visit, no deal.** Every qualified lead's mission is a site visit. Book it, confirm it, get the family there.
4. **No offer, no visit.** Every visit ends with an offer on the table — or it was a failed visit.
5. **90 days of follow-up, not 9.** The sale happens in week 6–12 for most buyers. Most competitors quit at week 4. That's where you win.
