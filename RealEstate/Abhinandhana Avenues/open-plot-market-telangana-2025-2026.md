## 3. Registration Values vs Market Values (Hyderabad)

- **Stamp duty stack:** 5% stamp duty + 1% registration + 0.5% cess + 0.1% surcharge, levied on **guidance (registry) value**, not market value [CONFIRMED] (facebook.com/HyderabadNewsinsta; vnctglobal.com).
- **Gap evidence (registry avg vs market):**
  - Kokapet: registry avg ₹9,076/sq ft (~₹81,700/sq yd) vs market ₹1.0–1.6 L/sq yd → **market ~1.2–2x registry** [CONFIRMED] (99acres.com/hmda-approved-plots-land-in-kokapet-hyderabad-ffid)
  - Narsingi: registry avg ₹9,066/sq ft (~₹81,600/sq yd) vs resale asks ₹45–60K/sq yd — in this zone registry can even exceed some market asks (older guidance) [CONFIRMED] (99acres.com/hmda-approved-plots-land-in-narsingi)
  - Shankarpally: registry avg only ₹2,242/sq yd vs market ₹25–45K/sq yd → **market ~11–20x registry** (typical of peri-urban converted layouts) [CONFIRMED] (99acres.com/residential-land-in-shankarpally-hyderabad-ffid)
- **2026 risk:** a ~**40% guidance-value hike** is in the pipeline; Hyderabad registrations **surged ~35% in May 2026** ahead of it [CONFIRMED] (landeed.com/post/telangana-land-prices-about-to-jump-40 ; instagram.com/p/DZhsO1iE_qL). Stamp duty cost rises when guidance catches up to market.

## 4. HMDA Approval Status — What Buyers Must Check

1. **Land conversion** — agricultural land converted to non-agricultural (revenue dept.) before layout [CONFIRMED] (youngindiahousing.com/blog/land-to-plot-telangana-explained, May 2026).
2. **Layout Permit (LP)** from HMDA (within HMDA jurisdiction) or DTCP (outside); Warangal layouts fall under **KUDA** (Kakatiya Urban Development Authority). Verify the LP number online — "HMDA approved" claims must trace to an issued LP [CONFIRMED] (srisuprajainfracon.com/blog/hmda-vs-dtcp-vs-rera, Jun 2026; gowraventures.com, Jul 2026).
3. **Zoning compliance (Master Plan 2031):** road widths, 10% open space, setbacks; plots **>1000 sq yd** trigger additional open-space/amenity obligations [CONFIRMED] (hmda.gov.in Zoning-Regulations-and-MasterPlan-GOs.pdf).
4. **RERA registration** (TS RERA) is mandatory for plotted developments above **500 sq m land or 8+ plots** — gives construction/possession timeline protection [CONFIRMED] (swargaseema.com/blog/why-rera-registration-matters-for-open-plots, Jul 2026).
5. **Typical checklist:** LP number, FTL/CRZ clearance if applicable, encumbrance, khata/title chain, NOC from ORR/airport authorities near corridors.

## 6. Key Takeaways

1. **Hyderabad** is a two-speed market: premium West (Kokapet–Tellapur, ₹65K–1.6L/sq yd) vs affordable South/fringe (Shamshabad–Shadnagar, ₹8.5–16K/sq yd); mid-market North (Kompally–Shamirpet ₹30–33K) is the value sweet spot.
2. **Warangal** offers entry at ₹4–18K/sq yd (fringe) with core Hanamkonda at ₹16–50K/sq yd — expect corridor-led 2–3x in 5–8 years if ORR + airport plans materialize.
3. **Registration gap** is widest in peri-urban layouts (Shankarpally: registry ~11–20x below market); a ~40% guidance hike in 2026 will raise transaction costs.
4. **Always verify:** HMDA/DTCP/KUDA Layout Permit + TS RERA registration + land conversion before purchase; gated HMDA+RERA layouts command 20–50% premiums over unapproved plots.
