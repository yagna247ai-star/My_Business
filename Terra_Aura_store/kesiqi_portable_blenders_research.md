### Certification Summary by Model
- **Most certified**: A7 and A11 2.0 — both carry CE, ROHS, LFGB, UKCA, FCC, FDA
- **LFGB certified**: A7, A11 2.0, A12 (basic), A12 (touch) — important for EU/German market food-contact compliance

- **REACH certified**: A12 (basic)
- **PSE certified**: A7 — Japan market
- **Basic certification only (CE, ROHS)**: A1 models, 6S, A6 (button), A9
- **No certifications listed**: 2000ML

### Pricing Observations
1. **Prices vary by region/currency** — Alibaba shows different currencies based on viewer location. All prices above are as displayed during research; actual USD prices may differ slightly.
2. **Sample pricing** is explicitly available on A1 (cover) at €10.53 and A12 (basic) at €12.29. Other models do not show a sample tier explicitly but "Get Sample" or 1pc ordering is likely available via supplier chat.
3. **Best value bulk model**: A7 (500ml) at ~£6.46/unit at 1000+ pcs with the most certifications.
4. **Lowest MOQ**: A6 (touchpad) at 50 pcs and 2000ML at 1 pc.

### Unique Differentiators
- **Highest power**: A9 at 270W
- **Largest battery**: A11 2.0 at 6000mAh (12V system)
- **Best materials**: A12 series uses Tritan (premium copolyester)
- **Most innovative**: A12 Touch — removable battery + touch screen display
- **Lowest entry price**: 6S model at ~THB 238/set for single-battery 2000mAh

- **Only 12V system**: A11 2.0 and A12 series
