# Sales Call Coach & Framework Skill

This skill acts as a coach and reviewer for sales calls, helping the team (specifically Yagna and Prabhuraj) master and execute the **4-Pillar Sales Call Framework**. It specializes in evaluating Fathom transcripts, generating custom pitches/scripts, and ensuring adherence to pricing anchors, closing strategies, and partner responsibilities.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Fathom Transcript & Recording Analysis**:
   - Analyzes shared Fathom call links or transcripts against the 4 Pillars.
   - Evaluates rapport time (goal: 90s non-business), diagnosis completeness (USP, current ad spend, pain points), automation system pitching (zero lead leakage, 7-day nurturing), and responsibility definition.

2. **Performance Scoring & Feedback**:
   - Generates scorecards for calls across the 4 pillars (0-100% execution score).
   - Identifies specific gaps, e.g., failure to anchor pricing, missing the 5-minute lead follow-up SLA explanation, or not offering the "Special Privilege Price" on-call.

3. **Script & Objection Handling Synthesis**:
   - Creates personalized pitch scripts matching specific customer niches (e.g., target audiences like software engineers).
   - Suggests response templates for common pricing objections using the anchoring strategy (₹50k standard vs. ₹25k privilege token advance).

## The 4-Pillar Sales Call Framework

### Pillar 1: Build Rapport (First 90 Seconds)
* **Goal**: Build a personal connection and establish initial credibility.
* **Key Actions**:
  * Spend the first 90 seconds discussing a non-business topic.
  * Ensure professional video presentation (dress code, clean background, camera angle).

### Pillar 2: Diagnose the Business
* **Goal**: Understand the client's history, pain points, and USP.
* **Key Actions**:
  * Ask exploratory questions to discover the client's "why" and business history.
  * Probe performance data: lead volume, sales numbers, and ad spend.
  * Identify their Unique Selling Proposition (USP) to frame the custom marketing strategy.

### Pillar 3: Present the Automation System
* **Goal**: Sell the "zero lead leakage" client acquisition system.
* **Key Components**:
  * **Ad Strategy**: Niche-specific, pain-point-focused ads (e.g., targeting software engineers).
  * **Nurturing Sequence**:
    * *Immediate Welcome*: Personalized WhatsApp/Email message with calendar booking link.
    * *7-Day Nurturing*: Client success stories sent to non-responders.
  * **Tools**: Integrated online calendar for scheduling and a CRM for pipeline/deal tracking.

### Pillar 4: Define Responsibilities
* **Goal**: Set clear, dual-sided partnership expectations.
* **Client Commitments**:
  * **Lead SLA**: Contact new leads within 5 minutes.
  * **Appointment Care**: Actively prioritize and nurture booked calls.
  * **Meta Ad Spend**: Budget ₹300–₹500/day paid directly to Meta.
  * **WhatsApp API Costs**: Budget ₹1,000–₹2,000/month paid directly to WhatsApp.
  * **Feedback Loop**: Report pipeline status in the shared WhatsApp group.

## Pricing & Closing Playbook

1. **Value Anchoring**:
   - Compare the program's value against standalone agency fees or custom tool subscriptions.
   - Position the 3-month program as the minimum timeframe to get reliable results.
2. **Offer Options**:
   - **Standard Price**: ₹50,000 for 3 months.
   - **Special Privilege Price**: ₹25,000 for 3 months (available *only* on the call via a token advance payment).
3. **Closing Strategy**:
   - Present two options: continue with current manual methods or invest in the system.
   - Use clear, transparent, and direct language to secure an on-call decision.
