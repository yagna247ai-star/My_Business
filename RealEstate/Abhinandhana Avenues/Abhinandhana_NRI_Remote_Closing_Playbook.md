## 1. NRI Buying Basics (FEMA) — Confirmed

| Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Can NRIs buy residential plots? | **YES.** NRIs/OCIs can buy any immovable property EXCEPT agricultural land, plantation property, farm house. Open residential plots in approved layouts = fully allowed. No RBI permission needed for NRI citizens; no filing required for NRIs buying under general permission (PIOs file Form IPI-7 within 90 days). | RBI Master Circular 4/2013-14 (FEMA 21/2000-RB, May 3, 2000) — rbi.org.in/commonman/english/scripts/Notification.aspx?Id=1175; mea.gov.in acquisition-transfer PDF |
| How must payment flow? | Only via (1) inward remittance through banking channels, or (2) debit to NRE / FCNR(B) / NRO accounts. **Prohibited:** traveller's cheques, foreign currency notes, cash (FEMA + PMLA). Token/EMI can be remitted directly to developer's bank account or from NRI's NRE/NRO. | RBI Master Circular §2A(iii); edgere.in guide (2026); calcwise.finance (2026) |
| Repatriation of sale proceeds | Two routes: (a) **USD 1M/financial year** from NRO balances / sale proceeds of rupee-funded or inherited assets (needs tax clearance/Form 15CA-CB); (b) up to the original forex consideration paid, for **max 2 residential properties**, if the sale is 3+ years after purchase (RBI FAQ for PIOs, Form IPI-8). Plot resale proceeds must be credited to NRO first, then remitted under the USD 1M facility. | RBI Master Circular §5A/§5B; RBI FAQ Q13–15 (registration.telangana.gov.in/faqsNRI.htm); assetlyhq.com (Mar 23, 2026) |

**Sales implication:** Since the NRO/USD-1M route works for rupee-funded purchases, NRIs can repatriate even plots bought via NRO funds — good FAQ material. Developers must accept payment ONLY via remittance/NRE-NRO debit and issue bank-routed receipts.

## 2. Power of Attorney (PoA) — The Critical Remote-Closing Mechanism

### Types
- **General PoA (GPA):** broad authority, multiple transactions. Avoid for property closing.
- **Special PoA (SPA):** limited, transaction-specific (e.g., "buy Plot 42, layout X, pay installments, execute & register sale deed"). **This is what NRIs should execute.**
- Registered vs notarized: an SPA executed abroad is notarized + attested/apostilled, then **stamped (adjudicated) at the Indian Sub-Registrar within 3 months of arrival in India**; SPAs that authorize the sale/conveyance of immovable property are compulsorily registrable under the Registration Act, 1908. For the **purchase** side, Telangana/AP sub-registrars in practice require the original stamped+registered PoA presented at registration. (nobroker.in May 18, 2026; 99acres; indiafornri.com; jurigram.com May 26, 2026)

### NRI PoA process (7 steps)
1. Lawyer/developer drafts SPA (buyer name, plot details, powers: pay token/EMIs, execute sale deed, register, receive documents).
2. NRI signs before a **Notary Public** in host country (US/UK/Australia/Gulf).
3. **Apostille** (Hague Convention countries — US, UK, Australia) at state/secy-of-state level, or **Indian Embassy/Consulate attestation** (non-Hague, e.g., some Gulf states) — takes 3–5 working days (kalpataru.com, Feb 12, 2026).
4. Courier original to India (3–7 days).
5. Stamp/adjudicate at Sub-Registrar within 3 months (Indian Stamp Act) — keshavaa.com Jun 12, 2026; nrilegalworld.com Jun 16, 2026.
6. Register the SPA if it involves sale/conveyance powers.
7. File POA copy + passport + address proof with developer/bank/SRO.

|---|---|---|

| **Total** | **~2–4 weeks end-to-end** | [ESTIMATED] |

### Can the PoA holder close on the NRI's behalf?
**YES — with conditions.** The attorney-in-fact can: sign the agreement-to-sell, pay token and EMIs (from NRI's NRO/NRE or remittance), execute the **sale deed** as the NRI's agent, pay stamp duty, appear at the Sub-Registrar and complete registration. Conditions: (a) SPA must explicitly grant these powers; (b) original stamped/registered PoA + NRI passport/visa copies; (c) title always vests in the **NRI**, never the PoA holder; (d) bank/developer must verify attorney identity (Aadhaar/PAN + photograph).

### Legal position (recent rulings — 2023–2026)
- **Suraj Lamp & Industries v. State of Haryana (2011)** line: GPA + Agreement-to-Sell + Will do **NOT** convey title — only a **registered sale deed** does.
- **M.S. Ananthamurthy v. J. Manjula (Feb 27, 2025; 2025 INSC 273)**: execution of a PoA is valid in law and not compulsorily registerable per se, but transfer of immovable property title requires a registered conveyance. (sci.gov.in PDF; legaleagleweb.com, Mar 3, 2025)
- **Ramesh Chand v. Suresh Chand (Sep 1, 2025)**: SC reiterated that agreement-to-sell/GPA — and even a registered will — cannot by themselves confer title (LiveLaw, Sep 2, 2025; SCC Online, Oct 7, 2025; Mondaq, Oct 10, 2025).
- **Practice rule for Abhinandhana:** NEVER sell on "GPA sale" model; PoA is only agency authorization. Always execute a **registered sale deed** with the PoA holder — this is also the #1 trust signal for NRI buyers.

### Remote registration alternatives (B6)
- No state (incl. AP/TS) currently offers full video-conference execution + registration of sale deeds for NRIs; **PoA remains the workhorse** [ESTIMATED].
- Telangana has NRI-specific registration guidance and e-services (registration.telangana.gov.in/faqsNRI.htm) and allowed NRIs to e-sign/gift agricultural land remotely via its portal (The Tribune, May 20, 2021) — signaling an e-sign-friendly registrar.
- **e-stamping + online challan** are fully available in TS/AP and let the PoA holder complete fee payment digitally (ujjivansfb.bank.in, Nov 29, 2025). Video-KYC/e-attestation is used by banks/developers for KYC but NOT as a substitute for PoA at the SRO [ESTIMATED].

## 3. How Top Developers Market Remote Closing (Mechanisms)

1. **Video site walks / drone walkthroughs:** master-project walkthroughs, aerial drone footage, 360° virtual tours of plots/sites — e.g., Godrej Brooklyn Avenues, Kukatpally publishes drone + 360° tours (godrejbrooklynsavenues.com); Hyderabad video firms (NS Ventures) produce locality + drone route videos; locality videos reportedly drive ~3× more walk-ins for remote buyers (nsventures.in).
2. **Live video calls with site manager:** scheduled WhatsApp/Google Meet calls during physical site visits — the salesperson walks plot boundaries, shows boundary pillars, neighbors, access roads, live.
3. **Family-member proxy visits:** NRIs' parents/siblings in India visit on their behalf; developers host dedicated "family site visit" sessions, WhatsApp the video to the NRI in real time, and record the visit. Joint ownership with resident relatives is common and legal (kalpataru.com, Jan 9, 2026); warn NRIs against buying in parents' names with NRI funds without documented structure (Benami Act risk — LinkedIn/CA commentary, 2025).
4. **Digital docs:** e-brochures, layout plans with QR markers, GPS-pinned plot location links, Dharani (TS) land-record screenshots shared over WhatsApp.

## 4. NRI-Specific Sales Practices

1. **Dedicated NRI desk / concierge:** end-to-end ownership — project discovery, virtual walkthroughs, PoA hand-holding, documentation, registration, possession, and post-sale updates (pattern: wycecorp.com NRI Hub; redbrik.com NRI Desk). Airport pickup and assisted registration visits when the NRI does travel.
2. **NRI exhibitions/meets:** Indian developers tour US (Seattle, Frisco), UK and Gulf cities — e.g., Dubai Property Expo circuit (NRI One, SVRE), India Property Expo, Telugu-targeted "Telugu Sweet Home" expo in Hyderabad (Jul 25–26, 2026); nrione.in runs NRI-focused expos.
3. **What NRI buyers care about (ranked):**
   1. **Title clarity & litigation-free plots** (parent documents, encumbrance, Dharani/ROR).
   2. **Approvals:** HMDA/DTCP (TS) or competent authority layout approvals + RERA registration — the single biggest filter for NRIs.
   3. **Trust/transparency:** track record, testimonials from other NRIs, physical office.
   4. **EMI flexibility** (token via remittance, no-EMI schemes) — EMI itself is a key differentiator.
   5. **Repatriation clarity** (USD 1M/yr NRO route) and resale/rental demand.
   6. **PoA ease** — developer's legal partner drafts the SPA free/at cost.
   7. **Appreciation:** 8–12% p.a. in growth corridors (Shadnagar, Kollur, Ibrahimpatnam etc. — klmprojects.in, 2026).
4. **Typical NRI decision timeline:** research 1–3 weeks → PoA 2–4 weeks → due diligence 1–3 weeks → token + agreement (same week as docs cleared) → installments/EMI → registration day → Dharani/patta mutation 2–6 weeks. **Realistic remote close: 4–10 weeks** from first call [ESTIMATED].

## 6. NRI Concern → Developer Solution Table

| "Can't visit the site" | Drone video, 360° tour, live WhatsApp walk with site manager, GPS-pinned plot links, recorded family-proxy visit |
