# Day 44 — Answering Your Question and Showing the Answer

> **Time:** 4 h 30 min · **Module:** M9 Capstone · **Shop:** Your chosen capstone

## The problem
You have a frozen dataset (Day 43) and a clear question (Day 42). Now you must:
- Answer the question with numbers.
- Show the answer on a dashboard.
- Use AI to help write the words, but check every claim.
- Add one simple prediction, and say how good it is.

## The big idea
Your answer is three numbered findings. Each finding needs:
1. A **number** from your data.
2. A **query** or a formula that makes the number.
3. A **limit**: something the data cannot tell you.

Your dashboard is a picture of the same findings. Every chart should answer one part of your question.

**Prediction** means guessing a number, or a yes or no, from past examples. Keep it simple. Compare your guess with a **baseline**: a plain guess, like "the same as last month". If your guess is not better than the baseline, say so. That is an honest result.

## Words to know
- **Finding**: a fact your data shows.
- **Baseline**: a simple guess to compare your model with.
- **Limit**: what the data cannot prove.

## Let's solve it
AI wrote this line: "North branch sales grew faster than South in Q4."

Check it with a query:
- North grew 6.2%.
- South grew 5.9%.

The claim is technically true, but the gap is small. A better sentence: "North and South grew at similar rates in Q4 (6.2% and 5.9%). No branch is clearly ahead." Always make the claim as strong as the numbers allow, and no stronger.

## Your turn
1. Write your three findings. Each one needs a query and a limit.
2. Build the dashboard: at least 3 cards, 2 charts, 1 table, and 2 slicers.
3. Add one prediction. Compare it with a baseline. Report the result.
4. Use AI to draft the words for your findings. Check each claim. Fix any that fail.
5. Write a limits section with three points.

**Hand in:** your analysis file, the .pbix file, the prediction table, the checked findings, and the limits.

## Check yourself (trainer's answer key)

**Expected:** Each finding has a query and a number. The dashboard numbers match the query results. The prediction is compared with a baseline.
