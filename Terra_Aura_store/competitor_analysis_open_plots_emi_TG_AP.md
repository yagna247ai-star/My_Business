## Key takeaways

1. **Two EMI models dominate:** in-house 0%-interest instalment schemes (24 Acres, Maaz City/A1, SV Constructions) for TG salaried buyers vs. bank-loan/agreement financing for AP gated communities (Alakananda, SRK, Amaravati Ventures).
2. **Price ladder:** entry ₹15,000–16,000/sq yd (Kothur/Shadnagar) → mid ₹18,000–25,000 (Shamirpet, Vijayawada) → premium ₹50,000+/sq yd (Aparna Serene Park resale).
3. **Marketing arms race:** Instagram reels + Facebook video ads + dedicated EMI landing pages + one phone number is the standard TG playbook; AP players invest in experience/events (Alakananda) and capital-region narrative (SRK, Amaravati Ventures).
4. **Trust levers everywhere:** HMDA/DTCP/RERA/CRDA approval numbers, RERA IDs, on-the-spot registration, and explicit "approved layout" language — every competitor leads with compliance.
