# Level 15 — AI Grandmaster · Persona Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Code | `L15_GRANDMASTER` · Stage 6, Career & Industry Readiness · 350 XP |
| Topics | Industry-sponsored challenges · Team hackathons · Advanced capstone · Working application · GitHub portfolio · Final demonstration · Career-readiness assessment |
| Unlock | Level 14 approved |
| Status | Spec only. **Industry sponsors aren't confirmed.** No sponsor names are listed until contracts exist; until then, the platform's challenge library supplies the challenges. Team mechanics use the existing hackathon module. |

## Shared and protected
**Outcomes.** The learner can:
- deliver an advanced capstone in a team
- ship a working application
- present a final demo
- hold a complete GitHub portfolio
- pass a career-readiness assessment

| # | Activity | Protected rules |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mission Briefing | Challenge selection: sponsor challenges when available, otherwise challenges from the library |
| 2 | Learn | 4 lessons: team roles and Git collaboration · scoping a capstone in a hackathon · demo storytelling · the career-readiness rubric. Quiz at 60% |
| 3 | Explore GitHub | Review 3 past capstone or showcase repos on the platform. 3 questions |
| 4 | Build: Team Capstone | A team of 2–5. A working application, a repo with commits from **every** member (each member's contribution is logged), tests, deployment and a README. The individual `contribution` statement is ≥80 words, and teammates confirm it |
| 5 | Submit Proof | Live URL, repo, a demo video of ≤5 minutes, and the GitHub portfolio link (all 15 levels' proofs indexed) |
| 6 | Evaluation and Boss | **Final demonstration** to a panel (instructor plus a mentor; plus a sponsor where one exists), and the **career-readiness assessment** (rubric below) |

**Proposed rubric** (individual score, even in a team):

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---|
| Working application | 0.20 |
| Technical or solution depth | 0.20 |
| Individual contribution (verified from commits and peers) | 0.15 |
| Demo and communication | 0.15 |
| Portfolio completeness | 0.15 |
| Career readiness (goal, evidence, next steps) | 0.15 |

Pass mark 3.0. Grandmaster certification goes only to learners who passed all 15 levels.

---

## Young Coder
- **Challenge type:** A "tech for school or community" problem, such as a library tracker, a club tool or a learning game.
- **Team:** Joining or creating a team needs verified guardian consent (enforced in `hackathon.ts`). Teams that include minors are moderated by an instructor. If consent is withdrawn, the learner is removed from open teams.
- **Final demo:** Shown to the instructor panel. A public showcase only with guardian consent; the demo can be shown without their face.
- **Career readiness for this group:** Reframed as **"learning readiness"**: a portfolio of 15 proofs, next learning goals, and a recommended next step (competitions, open-source docs work, school projects). The rubric dimension is the same; the evidence is age-appropriate.
- **Safety:** Under-18 talent profiles are never discoverable, and under-18s can't apply to opportunities, even with guardian consent (enforced in `talent.ts`; `CHILD_SAFETY_POLICY.md` §3).

## Student & Career Starter
- **Challenge type:** Industry-style problems, sponsored where available.
- **Team:** Cross-skill (developer, product, data).
- **Final demo:** A 5-minute demo plus Q&A, recorded for the portfolio.
- **Career readiness:**
  - a resume that links every claim to a proof
  - a mock interview with a mentor
  - a target role list matched to the track
  - talent-marketplace visibility, opt-in
- **Output:** Showcase entry plus a "hire me" portfolio page.

## Engineering Professional
- **Challenge type:** An enterprise-grade problem, such as a multi-tenant agent, a RAG system with compliance needs, or a reliability problem.
- **Team:** Lead or architect role encouraged. The `contribution` statement must show design ownership, not only code.
- **Final demo:** An architecture walk-through, live demo, eval results and cost.
- **Career readiness:** A promotion or role-change case (internal or external), a public technical write-up, and optionally conference or meetup talk notes.

## AI Builder, Enthusiast
- **Challenge type:** Build a product others can use, judged on real usage.
- **Team:** Builder plus at least one coder or designer.
- **Final demo:** A product demo, user numbers with how they were measured, and the roadmap.
- **Career readiness:** The path chosen (freelance, startup, job), with a plan for the next steps (niche, offer, first clients). No income claims are made or promised.

## Business Owner
- **Challenge type:** Their own business transformation, or a challenge set by a fellow owner.
- **Team:** Owner plus a builder or developer learner (cross-persona teams are encouraged).
- **Final demo:** Before and after operations (hours, response time, errors) from their own run logs, plus the risk controls.
- **Career readiness for this group:** Reframed as **"business readiness"**: a 90-day AI roadmap for the business, a budget with dated price sources, a staff-training plan, and the controls that stay in place. The rubric dimension is the same.
