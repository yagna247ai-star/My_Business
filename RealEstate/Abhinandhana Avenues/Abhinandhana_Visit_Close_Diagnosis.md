## Why 98% of Visitors Attend the Site but 0% Book — Root Cause & Fix Playbook

## Executive Summary — The Diagnosis in One Page

**The 98%-attend / 0%-book pattern is the signature of TWO failures stacked on top of each other:**

1. **The traffic is largely unqualified free-visit traffic.** The AP/TS open-plot market runs on a "free experience" funnel — Telugu-language ads promising ఉచిత సైట్ విజిట్ (free site visit), free door-to-door pickup/drop, free lunch, free bus trips, free gifts. When the visit costs the visitor nothing (no commitment, no deposit, free transport + food), the funnel removes its own filter: **everyone who confirms shows up** (hence 98% attendance) — but the majority are outing attendees, not buyers.

   > Industry benchmark: **qualified traffic converts 15–25% of site visits into bookings** (25–35% with CRM); plot-specific: 8–12% average, 20–25% top performers **[CONFIRMED — realatic.com, Apex Influence]**. Your ~0% sits 15–35 points below the qualified-visit benchmark. The visitor population is not qualified by any standard definition.

2. **There is likely no structured close process at the site.** Top-converting teams use a 5-stage closing ritual (warm welcome → personalized tour → price after value → micro-urgency → **blocking offer**). Zero conversion across ~98% attendance means the visit is ending with "we'll be in touch" instead of a blocking offer, no on-the-spot EMI math, and no documents proactively shown. **[CONFIRMED — Realtomation]**

**Therefore: the fix is two-sided — (A) qualify traffic BEFORE offering the free visit, and (B) install the closing process AT the site and in the 48-hour post-visit window.** Both are fixable within 30 days. This is a process problem, not a market problem.

**Structural rule [ESTIMATED]:** A lead source whose creative promise is *free experience* (free visit, free lunch, free pickup) delivers attendees whose motivation is the experience. A source whose creative promise is *investment facts / price / approvals* delivers buyers. **The same media channel can produce either — it depends on the creative and the lead form.** A 2-line "book free visit" form = outing traffic. A form asking budget + purpose + timeline = buyer traffic.

### 2.2 The 2-Minute Qualification Gate (Ask BEFORE the tour, not after lunch)

Ask these in the first 2 minutes after greeting. **Visitors who fail ≥3 of 8 gates = outing/emissary — serve basic tour + lunch (keep the promise, protect reputation) but do NOT give pricing brochures, EMI sheets, or senior salesperson time. Tag them "outing" in the CRM.**

| # | Question | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "How did you hear about us — Instagram, YouTube, friend, or walk-in?" | Source tag; flags un-trackable "somewhere" answers |
| 2 | "Who is this plot for — you and your family? Will your family see it today?" | Decision-maker present or emissary? |
| 3 | "Have you thought about a budget range — around how much per sq.yd or total?" | Buyers have a number; outing attendees don't |
| 4 | "Are you planning to build and live there, or is this an investment plot?" | End-use vs investment vs no-answer |
| 5 | "Which area do you currently stay in? Are you looking in this direction?" | Serious buyers have a geography |
| 6 | "When are you thinking of completing the purchase — this month, 3 months, or just exploring?" | **The single strongest intent filter** |
| 7 | "Have you seen other layouts recently, or spoken to a bank for finance?" | Comparison stage + financial readiness |
| 8 | **Document test:** "Before we walk the site, let me show you the approvals and title file — 5 minutes." | Real buyers engage; outing attendees lose interest |

### 2.3 Red Flags of Free-Visit / Unqualified Traffic

**Pre-visit (on the booking call):**
1. First question is about **lunch / gifts / transport / freebies** — not the plot, price, or location
2. **No budget question** in 3–5 minutes; cannot state any range when asked ("budget ante... chudali")
3. **Never asks about approvals** — DTCP/RERA/HMDA/CRDA, title, patta, encumbrance ("approved ah?" is the #1 question real AP/TS buyers ask)
4. Cannot name the ad or project ("I saw it somewhere on Instagram")
5. Asks to bring a large group (friends/cousins) rather than spouse/family decision-makers
6. Phone number **not on WhatsApp**, or name mismatch
7. No timeline: "one day", "just checking", "tension lo unnam" [CONFIRMED category — "tire-kickers" fail on budget/timeline/decision power — getnextphone.com]

**On-site:**
8. First actions: checking food, family selfies, picnic behavior; kids running; no one engages with the layout
9. **Refuses to engage with documents** — eyes glaze over when the approval/title file opens
10. **Family-size mismatch**: says "small family" but arrives with 5–8 people (outing-sized, not decision-sized)
11. Says **"will bring family next time"** — and repeats across visits without the decision-maker ever arriving
12. No follow-through: promised ID proof/bank details, never sends; no reply to follow-ups
13. Serial site-trippers: visits multiple projects every weekend with the same group
14. Asks "what will you give if I book today" (gift-led, not value-led)

## Part 3: Why Visitors Walk After Visiting — The Real Reasons (Research-Backed)

### 3.1 The Five Profiles Behind "We'll Think About It"

| # | Profile | Behavioral tell | Share estimate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Outing attendee** — no intent ever existed; free trip + lunch was the point | Took the lunch and selfies; zero substantive questions; only speaks when addressed | Large (dominant if traffic is Meta/YouTube free-visit) [ESTIMATED] |
| 2 | **Emissary** — decision-maker (spouse/parent/NRI) never came; visitor can't decide | "I'll discuss with my wife/parents", "bring them next time" — and never does | Significant for NRI-belt leads [ESTIMATED] |
| 3 | **Price shock** — advertised low entry (₹2–3L down, EMI framing) vs real total (plot + ~7.5% registration + development charges) creates silent sticker shock | No objection voiced, but goes cold; won't re-engage on price | Contributing [CONFIRMED ads; ESTIMATED behavior] |
| 4 | **Trust doubt** — AP/TS plot scams are front-page: ₹300+ crore pre-launch township scam (Hyderabad), corner-plot scams; buyers verify the company online after the visit | Asks about approvals once, then disengages; checks company registration online afterwards | Contributing [CONFIRMED — SumanTV Mix, Reddit r/hyderabad] |
| 5 | **Comparison shopper** — has 2–3 more free visits lined up next weekend | Polite, engaged, asks all the right questions — then vanishes; surfaces weeks later from another project | Contributing [CONFIRMED — buyers visit multiple projects; 3–7 projects benchmark] |

### 3.2 Decoding "We'll Think About It" — The Honest Translation

- **"We'll think" ≈ "No" 70–80% of the time** — especially for outing attendees and emissaries; it's the socially-safe "no" in Indian sales culture [ESTIMATED]
- **~15–25% of the time it means "not convinced yet"** — these are the convertable visitors (matches the 15–25% qualified visit→booking benchmark) but ONLY with structured follow-up [CONFIRMED benchmark; ESTIMATED decode]
- **The unspoken subtexts to probe in follow-up:**
  - "We'll think" → *ask:* "Is it the price, the approval, or the family that's pending?" (force the real objection)
  - "Will bring family" → *ask:* "Shall I block the plot for Saturday — what time suits your wife?" (commitment-test the promise)
  - "Just checking rates" → tag as outing/cold; move to nurture; don't spend senior time
  - **Silence after visit → the 5-touch cadence decides:** if a genuinely qualified visitor (budget+timeline+decision power) doesn't respond to 5 touches, treat as lost to comparison/price — and interrogate the price-vs-value story, not the visitor

### 3.3 The Six Developer-Fault Reasons Visits Don't Close (Not the Visitor's Fault)

**[CONFIRMED — Realtomation S1/S4/S6]** — these are the process gaps that, combined with 98% attendance, produce ~0%:

1. **No close attempt made on site** — visit ends with "we'll be in touch" instead of a blocking offer
2. **Salesperson asks for a decision, then goes silent** — "so, will you book?" with no anchored next step, no scheduled follow-up
3. **No urgency** — everything appears available forever; buyers need a dated, real reason (price revision date, "2 plots left in corner zone")
4. **No EMI math done on the spot** — fatal for an EMI-plot business: the buyer leaves without knowing "₹6,800/month × 60 months" fits their budget. **The EMI script IS the close for this product.**
5. **No documents shown** — legal fear is the #1 delay cause; if approvals/EC/title are only available on request, risk-averse buyers walk
6. **No post-visit follow-up system** — 50%+ of buyers need 6–12 follow-ups; bookings mostly close after Day 7; **only 10–15% of sales teams follow up beyond 2 attempts** — that statistic alone explains most ghosting

## Part 4: What Top-Converting Developers Do Differently at the Site Visit

### 4.1 The 5-Stage Closing Ritual (High-Performer Standard)

**[CONFIRMED — Realtomation S1/S5/S6]** — this is the on-site process that lifts visit→booking from 8–20% to 32–51%:

**Stage 1 — Warm Welcome (first 3 minutes, NOT a sales pitch)**
- Offer water/tea; learn the names of ALL family members present; thank them for the commute
- Do NOT ask budget immediately; do NOT push booking early

**Stage 2 — Personalized tour (never the same tour for everyone)**
- Family with children → school connectivity, safety, parks
- Elderly members → medical access, calm surroundings, walkable layout
- Investor → appreciation data, price trends, resale liquidity, micro-market history
- **Open plots specifically: show the actual plot they'd buy — walk them onto it, stand on it, show boundary pegs — not just "the layout."** Physical boundary/demarcation proof removes legal-physical doubt [ESTIMATED from S8]

**Stage 3 — Price reveal AFTER value (the #1 timing rule)**
- Correct order: **Lifestyle → location → approvals → amenities → convenience → trust → THEN price**
- "Pricing depends on plot size, road-facing, and zone — I'll show you the best-matching options in your budget once you see which plots you prefer." Price then feels logical, not shocking
- **Revealing price too early is called "the biggest conversion mistake"** [CONFIRMED]

**Stage 4 — Micro-urgency without pressure (priority, not push)**
- "Corner plots facing the park are the fastest selling — if you like this one, let me check exact availability before someone else takes it."
- Use genuine urgency only (inventory truth, dated price revisions); fake urgency is detected and destroys trust [CONFIRMED]

**Stage 5 — Blocking offer instead of booking ask**
- NEVER lead with "do you want to book?" Ask: **"Would you like to block this plot for 24 hours so no one else takes it while you finalize with family?"**
- Blocking = low resistance; booking = high resistance. Once blocked, the buyer is mentally committed → high booking probability. **Many bookings happen AFTER blocking.**
- Commitment ladder: plot blocking → refundable token → 24–72h price protection → booking [CONFIRMED S6]

### 4.2 On-Site Aids That Close (Checklist for Your Site Office)

| Element | Why it closes | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Model/site office with welcome desk** | Signals an organized permanent developer; venue for the close | [ESTIMATED] |
| **Large layout plan board (physical, on site)** | Buyer visualizes their exact plot, road, park, neighbors | [CONFIRMED] |
| **Boundary pegs on the actual plot** | Buyer "owns" the plot emotionally when standing in it | [ESTIMATED] |
| **Infrastructure demo** (black-topped roads, drainage, streetlights, compound wall, avenue plantation) | Infrastructure quality is the single strongest differentiator and primary appreciation driver | [CONFIRMED — Apex] |
| **Documents wall** — approval order, EC, title chain, layout plan displayed | Proactively show, don't wait to be asked; trust anxiety → confidence | [CONFIRMED] |
| **Seating/negotiation room** with price list + EMI chart + booking form pre-positioned | The blocking conversation happens here; a chair converts better than a doorstep | [ESTIMATED] |
| **Photos/videos of recent family bookings** | Social proof: "3 families booked last week" | [CONFIRMED] |
| **EMI calculator sheet on the table** | On-the-spot EMI math is the close for this product | [CONFIRMED] |

### 4.3 Offer & Token Discipline

- **Token norms: 2–5% of plot value** for plotted development [CONFIRMED — Apex]; market examples: ₹11,000 booking on low-cost plots; EMI-plot sellers take ₹20,000–1,00,000 [CONFIRMED]. **For your ₹3–10L ticket sizes: ₹11,000–50,000 token** [ESTIMATED]
- **RERA refund rules:** developer-cancellation = full refund + interest (SBI MCLR + 2%) within 45 days; buyer-cancellation forfeiture capped ~2% (MahaRERA cap) [CONFIRMED]
- **EMI presentation (the decisive tactic for your product):** present payment as "pay like rent" — e.g., ₹4.5L plot → ₹90,000 down → **₹6,500–7,000/month for 2–5 years, registration on completion**. Do the math on the spot in writing. Pair with appreciation story ("Shadnagar ₹2L in 2020 → ₹5L in 2026") [CONFIRMED — Truly Acres S9]
- **Deal-killers to ban from your site:** pushing booking too early; revealing price before value; discount-first (erodes trust — high performers are low-discount teams); ignoring elder family members; skipping the blocking option; weak close ("so what do you think?"); no follow-up; fake urgency [CONFIRMED]

### 4.4 Handling the Absent Decision-Maker (NRI / Family) — Two-Audience Selling

The classic AP/TS pattern: **a family member physically visits; the NRI decides later from abroad.** Top developers treat the visit as a **two-audience event** — sell the family on-site AND sell the NRI remotely in the same visit [CONFIRMED — Apex, Kalpataru]:

1. **Live video call with the NRI during/right after the visit** — walk the actual plot with the camera, show the layout board, let the NRI ask questions live [CONFIRMED — Kalpataru]
2. **WhatsApp document pack within hours** — RERA/DTCP approval copy, layout plan, price sheet, EMI plan, encumbrance certificate [CONFIRMED]
3. **POA hand-holding** — notarized + apostilled POA to a trusted resident Indian; NRI completes entire purchase remotely [CONFIRMED — Kalpataru, ICICI]
4. **Compliance certainty** — payments only via NRE/NRO/FCNR; residential plots allowed without RBI approval; 1% TDS on purchases >₹50L. Sell this certainty — it's the NRI's biggest anxiety [CONFIRMED]
5. **Time-zone-aware follow-up** — evening IST calls for Gulf/US NRIs [CONFIRMED]

### 4.5 The 48-Hour Post-Visit Protocol (Most Bookings Happen Here, Not On-Site)

| Timing | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Thank-you + **video of the SPECIFIC plot they liked** (walked by the site exec) + recap of what they liked |
| Day 2 | Inventory update with real counts ("only 2 plots left in that zone — should I recheck availability?") |
| Day 5 | Soft urgency: dated price revision / scheme deadline |
| Day 10 | **EMI sheet computed for their budget** — exact numbers, not estimates |
| Day 15+ | Low-frequency nurture: social-proof videos of family bookings, infrastructure progress video, festive scheme reminders |

**Cadence rules [CONFIRMED]:** 80% of sales need 5+ follow-ups; **50%+ of buyers need 6–12 follow-ups over 30–90 days**; bookings mostly close after Day 7 (7–45 days typical). Every WhatsApp message ends with a question ("morning or evening slot?") — personalization lifts response ~70%.

## Appendix A: Practical Telugu Scripts for Sales Warriors (Ground Level)

### A1. The Booking-Call Qualification (Before Offering the Free Visit)
> "నమస్తే సార్/మేడమ్! మా Abhinandhana Avenues నుండి. మీరు ప్లాట్ గురించి విచారించారు కదా.
> ఒక్కటి తెలుసుకోవాలనుకుంటున్నాను —
> 1) మీరు ఎలా తెలుసుకున్నారు — ఇన్స్టాగ్రామ్, యూట్యూబ్, ఫ్రెండ్, లేదా నేరుగా వాక్-ఇన్?
> 2) ఈ ప్లాట్ మీ కోసమా, ఇన్వెస్ట్మెంట్ కోసమా? (Purpose)
> 3) మీ బడ్జెట్ ఎంత వరకు ఆలోచిస్తున్నారు — స్క్వేర్ యార్డుకి లేదా మొత్తం? (Budget)
> 4) ఎప్పుడు కొనాలనుకుంటున్నారు — ఈ నెలలో, మూడు నెలల్లో, లేదా ఇప్పుడే చూస్తున్నారా? (Timeline)
> 5) కుటుంబం కూడా వస్తుందా? ఎవరు నిర్ణయం తీసుకుంటారు? (Decision-maker)"

**Decision rule:** budget + timeline + decision-maker confirmed → offer the visit. Missing ≥2 → don't offer free transport/lunch; send a price brochure instead.

### A2. The Site Visit Close (5-Stage, Telugu)

**Welcome (Stage 1):**
> "రండి రండి సార్! మీరు వచ్చినందుకు చాలా సంతోషం. ఇవ్వండి — ముందు నీళ్ళు/టీ తాగండి. అమ్మానాన్న కూడా వచ్చారా? అందరి పేర్లు తెలుసుకోవచ్చా?"

**After the tour, BEFORE price (Stage 3):**
> "సార్, ఇక్కడ ముఖ్యమైన విషయాలు చెప్పాలి — ఈ లేఅవుట్ DTCP/HMDA అప్రూవ్ అయింది, RERA రిజిస్ట్రేషన్ ఉంది, ఎన్కంబరెన్స్ క్లియర్, టైటిల్ క్లీన్. ఇవన్నీ డాక్యుమెంట్స్ ఇక్కడే చూపిస్తాను. మీరు కోరుకున్న ప్లాట్ దగ్గర నిలబడి చూడండి — ఇదే మీది అనిపిస్తుంది."

**Price reveal after value:**
> "ఈ ప్లాట్ మీ బడ్జెట్కి సరిపోయేలా చూద్దాం. స్క్వేర్ యార్డ్ రేటు, రోడ్ ఫేసింగ్, కార్నర్ ప్లాట్ అన్నిటి బట్టి ఉంటుంది. మీకు నచ్చిన ప్లాట్ చూపిస్తాను."

**The Blocking Offer (Stage 5) — the key close:**
> "సార్, నిజం చెప్పాలంటే — ఈ జోన్లో కార్నర్ ప్లాట్లు ముందుగా అమ్ముడవుతాయి. మీరు ఇష్టపడ్డ ప్లాట్ ఈరోజు బ్లాక్ చేస్తే, 24 గంటలు మీ కోసం పెట్టుకుంటాం — ఎవరికీ ఇవ్వం, ధర కూడా అలాగే ఉంటుంది. మీ కుటుంబంతో చర్చించి నిర్ణయం తీసుకోండి. బుకింగ్ కాదు — కేవలం హోల్డ్. మీ ID కాపీ ఇస్తే సరిపోతుంది."

**EMI math on the spot (for the EMI product):**
> "మీరు ఈ ప్లాట్ తీసుకుంటే — డౌన్ పేమెంట్ X, తర్వాత నెలకి ₹Y, Z నెలలు. అంటే నెలకి ఒక రెంటు కట్టినట్టు. రిజిస్ట్రేషన్ పూర్తి పేమెంట్ తర్వాత. ఇదుగో లెక్క — ఇక్కడే కూర్చుని చూడండి. మీ ఇంటి ఖర్చులకి తగ్గట్టు ఎలా సర్దుకుంటుందో లెక్క పెడదాం."

### A3. Handling "We'll Think About It" (Decode the Objection)
> "సార్, అర్థమైంది. కానీ ఒక్కటి చెప్పండి — మీ ఆలోచనలో మూడు విషయాల్లో ఏది పెండింగ్? ధర, అప్రూవల్స్, లేదా కుటుంబం? నేను మీకు అవసరమైన సమాచారం ఇస్తాను. మీరు మా ఫోన్లో ఇప్పుడే చెప్పగలిగితే, మీ ఇంట్లో అందరికీ చూపించే ఒక పేపర్ కూడా సిద్ధం చేస్తాను — తెలుగులో."
