### POST 2.2 — Plot Selection Guide #1: The 5 Documents Before You Pay (Sat)

**Theme:** plot selection guidance
**Format:** checklist card (image) + text

**English:**
> **Never pay token money before checking these 5 documents.** (Works for any plot, any state.)
>
> 1. **Layout Approval / LP number** — Telangana: HMDA or DTCP layout permit; AP: APCRDA/VMRDA/TUDA or DTCP LP number. "Approved" claims must trace to a real LP number — verify online ([HMDA](https://hmda.gov.in), [DTCP AP](https://dtcp.ap.gov.in), [APCRDA](https://crda.ap.gov.in))
> 2. **RERA registration** — mandatory for plotted developments >500 sqm or >8 plots in both states; verify on [rera.telangana.gov.in](https://rera.telangana.gov.in) / [rera.ap.gov.in](https://rera.ap.gov.in)
> 3. **Title chain** — Telangana: Dharani record; AP: Adangal + 30-year title chain; advocate title opinion for AP (title-litigation-heavy) [VERIFY: with your lawyer]
> 4. **Encumbrance Certificate (EC)** — from Sub-Registrar; proves no loans/litigation on the land
> 5. **Land conversion** — agricultural land must be converted to non-agricultural (TS: layout approval process / LRS-2020; AP: Agricultural Land Conversion Act 2006, ~10% conversion fee commonly cited [VERIFY current %])
>
> **Golden rule:** if a seller cannot produce these 5 in 24 hours, slow down. A clean plot is worth more than a cheap plot with a hidden problem.
>
> **Save this card** — it is your first filter before any site visit.

### POST 8.2 — Resale Liquidity: How to Judge If a Plot Will SELL Later (Sat)

**Theme:** plot enhancements / investment logic
**Format:** voice note (90 sec) + checklist

**English:**
> **"Can I sell this plot in 5 years?" — the question every investor should ask. The 5 liquidity tests:**
>
> 1. **Approvals:** HMDA/DTCP/CRDA-approved plots have a resale market; unapproved ones do not
> 2. **Active corridor:** plots near working infrastructure (airport, IT, highway) resell faster than isolated ones
> 3. **Comparable transactions:** check 3–5 recent registered sales in the same layout/mandal — that is your future exit price
> 4. **Inventory overhang:** how many unsold plots remain in the layout? High overhang = weak resale
> 5. **Small denominations:** 100–200 sq yd plots resell easier than large 500+ sq yd ones (bigger pool of buyers)
>
> **Why registration values matter here:** areas where guidance ≈ market (e.g., Kakinada Ramanayyapeta, Nellore city — guidance close to asking) have more transparent resale pricing ([assestlyhq IGRS FY25-26](https://assestlyhq.com))
>
> **Rule:** you buy at a price, but you sell at a *market* — judge the exit market before the entry price.
>
> **Save this** — apply the 5 tests to any plot you are considering.
