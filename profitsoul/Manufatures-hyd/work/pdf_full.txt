Why manufacturers are the gold mine. 
They already pay ₹30k to ₹2L a year to IndiaMART and JustDial for enquiries, then let those enquiries 
rot. They think in crores, not in ₹5k software line items. They do not want five vendors — they want 
one person who runs everything and stays. An experienced technical person like Yagna reads to 
them as a peer, same age and mindset, so trust is faster. Three retained manufacturers = ₹24k–
₹30k/month recurring that renews without a new sale. 
The 30–35% rule — read this before blaming lead quality. 
Meta is an impulse audience, not an intent audience: a manufacturer is watching reels and our ad 
interrupts. So 60–65% of every 100 enquiries will always recycle — irrelevant or future-only — for us, 
for SaaS Universe, for anyone running Meta. That is not a targeting failure; it is how the platform 
works. The money is hidden in the 30–35% who actually book and show for the call. At ₹10k spend 
→ ~100 enquiries → even 2 closing at ₹15k = ₹30k = 3x. So the entire job from here is converting the 
ones who book, not chasing lead volume. Which makes this a sales-call problem, not an ads problem 
— and the sales-call training below is the real lever. 
Qualifier. 
Real business owners only. Manufacturers already paying for IndiaMART / JustDial, or already 
running enquiries. Random “I'm into a different business” clickers are disqualified on the call, not 
sold to.

The reframe that makes it land:  
“I'm not asking you to trust me, I'm asking you to test me.” Manufacturers are experienced and 
sceptical — a test at ₹2,500 beats a pitch at any price. And always close on the long-term 
relationship, because that is the one thing the ₹5k “software only” vendors cannot offer. 
Delivery scope 
Read this before any sales call. Promise only what is on the “we build” list. The ₹2,500 is a trial of a 
running system, not a custom software project. 
What we build in the 14 days 
• Instant automated WhatsApp reply on every enquiry (IndiaMART, JustDial, WhatsApp) 
• Automated follow-up and reminder sequences so no buyer is forgotten 
• CRM pipeline + dashboard showing every enquiry and where it stopped 
• Calendar automation for appointments and callbacks 
• Task management and a file vault 
• A manufacturer website / form, revamped via Cloud (not hand-coded) 
What we do NOT promise 
• We do not run their Meta ads. The ₹2,500 is software and automation only. 
• No bespoke custom-built website. The revamp is Cloud / Astra generated — fast and clean, but 
not a hand-built dev project. Say “revamp”, never “custom build”. 
• No AI conversation agent qualifying buyers in chat. The WhatsApp reply is an automated flow; a 
human follows up. 
Consequence for the sales call. 
The ad promises a reply in 5 seconds — WhatsApp automation delivers that and it is true. It does not 
promise us running their ads, or a from-scratch website. On the call, when a manufacturer asks “can 
you revamp my old website?” the answer is yes — via Cloud, inside the system — not yes to an 
unpaid custom build. Keep the fence, or the ₹2,500 trial becomes free consulting. 
The 5-second claim is only real if a human is fast. 
Every booked lead gets a callback within 5 minutes of landing, inside calling hours. The automation 
buys the first reply; Yagna wins the deal on the follow-up.
