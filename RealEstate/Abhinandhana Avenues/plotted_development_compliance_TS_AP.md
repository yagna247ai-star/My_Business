# RERA, Legal & Stamp Duty Research — Plotted Developments (Open Plots)
## Telangana vs Andhra Pradesh (compiled Aug 5, 2026)

**Scope:** Open-plot / plotted-development projects (layout sales). Labels: ✅ **Confirmed** (multiple/official sources) vs ⚠️ **Verify with lawyer/consultant** (conflicting, dated, or unofficial data).

---

# SECTION 1 — TELANGANA

## A) RERA (Telangana Real Estate Regulatory Authority — TG RERA)

**1. Is registration mandatory for plotted developments?** ✅ YES for projects with land area **>500 sqm OR >8 units/plots** (Central RERA Act s.3, adopted by TS). ✅ Confirmed by TG RERA exemptions text: all commercial/residential projects must register except small-area projects. ⚠️ **Feb 2026 TG RERA Appellate Tribunal clarification:** projects on plots **below ~500 sq yd (~418 sqm)** exempt from mandatory registration (small-project relaxation) — confirm exact sq-yd threshold and whether it applies to your plot sizes before relying on it.

**2. Registration process, fees, timelines**
- ✅ Online at rera.telangana.gov.in; certificate issued within ~30 days of valid application (Rules 2017).
- ✅ Project fee per sqm of land: **₹5/sqm** (project ≤1,000 sqm), **₹10/sqm** (1,001–2,000 sqm), **₹20/sqm** (above — slab per TG RERA Rules 2017 fee schedule). ⚠️ Confirm exact slab boundaries; plus late-fee charges (TG RERA circular 04/03/2025 on fee/late-fee implementation).
- Required docs: title deed, approved layout/plan, sanction letter, balance sheet/P&L, project timelines, promoter PAN.

**3. Penalties for non-registration** ✅
- Section 59 RERA: penalty **up to 10% of estimated project cost** (+ continuing daily penalty; possible imprisonment for persistent violations).
- Enforcement examples: ✅ **₹38.63 lakh** fine on Indo Qatar Projects (June 2026: ₹30.66L unregistered projects + ₹7.97L agent violation); ✅ **₹25.8 lakh** for illegal farm-plot sales (Oct 2025); ✅ **₹14.91 lakh** for non-registration + refund with 11% interest (May 2025); ✅ Agents penalized for selling in unregistered "Amber Homes" project (Aug 2026). Also flat **₹2 lakh** penalty cited for projects approved 01.01.2017–31.08.2018.

**4. Registered plotted-development projects** — TS ranks **#4 in India** in RERA registrations (2022 data: ~2,200+ projects, 151 agents — dated). ⚠️ Query live registry (rera.telangana.gov.in → Search Registered Projects) for current plotted-layout count.

**5. Recent orders affecting plotted developments** ✅
- Feb 2026: Tribunal exemption clarification for sub-500 sq yd plot projects.
- 2025–26: aggressive penalty trend against unregistered plot sales and agents (fines above).
- 2025: TG RERA extension of registration validity for eligible projects (circular).

**6. RERA agent registration (sales teams — CRITICAL)** ✅
- **Mandatory** before facilitating any sale; valid **5 years**, renewable.
- Fees: **Individual ₹10,000; Company/Firm ₹50,000** (TG RERA Rules 2017).
- Docs: PAN, annual business report, P&L, balance sheet.

## B) Legal requirements for plot sale

**1. Layout approval**
- ✅ **HMDA** (Hyderabad Metropolitan area) or **DTCP** (rest of state) layout permission required before plot sales; Gram-Panchayat-only layouts are NOT valid in HMDA area (HMDA FAQ).
- ⚠️ DTCP layout minimums cited: ~1 acre (4,000 sqm) and 30 m width — verify per current rules; **LRS-2020 (Layout Regularization Scheme)**: unapproved layouts/subdivisions existing as of 26-Aug-2020 with ≥10% plots sold can be regularized.

**2. Agricultural land conversion**
- ✅ Approved layouts get conversion to non-agricultural use through the layout-permission process; unapproved/illegal subdivisions eligible via **LRS-2020** (Telangana Building Permission Approval & Self-Certification Act, 2020 — Act 12 of 2020). ⚠️ Document name ("D-Formal"/D-FC) and RDO/MRO procedure vary by district — verify with local revenue office.

**3. Mutation, encumbrance, title** ✅ — Title/ownership & mutation via **Dharani** (TS land records portal); **Encumbrance Certificate (EC)** from Registration & Stamps Dept; verify 30-year chain + no-encumbrance before agreement.

**4. Agreement of sale vs sale deed** ✅ — Agreement of sale (registered, with token advance) does NOT transfer title; title passes only on **registered sale deed**. For plots, both executed at Sub-Registrar on market/guideline value.

**5. State-specific rules** ✅ — Telangana Layout Rules 2020 (Act 12 of 2020), LRS-2020 (G.O. 62 MA&UD 21.03.2020), TS RERA Rules 2017.

# SECTION 2 — ANDHRA PRADESH

## A) RERA (AP RERA)

**1. Mandatory for plotted developments?** ✅ YES — same Central Act test: **>500 sqm OR >8 units (plots), including all phases** (AP RERA Rules 2017 / AP rules summary). Below-threshold projects exempt.

**2. Process, fees, timelines**
- ✅ Online at rera.ap.gov.in; **registration certificate within ~30 days** of complete application.
- ⚠️ Project fee: per AP RERA Rules 2017 schedule (per-sqm slabs similar to TS) — exact current slabs **verify**; agent filing services quoted from ~₹6,000 (rerafiling.com) but statutory fee differs.

**3. Penalties** ✅ — Section 59: up to **10% of estimated project cost**; daily penalty for continuing default. ⚠️ AP enforcement record is thinner than TS — check aprera.gov.in orders/penalty register for plotted-layout cases.

**4. Registered projects** ⚠️ — "**Over 4,000** registered projects" state-wide (99acres, Feb 2024, dated). Query live registry (rera.ap.gov.in → Search Projects) for current plotted-development count.

**5. Recent orders** ⚠️ — No major 2025–26 TS-style plotted-development crackdowns surfaced in searches; AP RERA activity focused on compliance reporting. Verify with local counsel.

**6. Agent registration** ⚠️ — Mandatory before facilitating sales (5-yr validity, per Act). Fee structure per AP Rules 2017 — likely **₹10,000 individual / ₹50,000 firm** (mirroring TS) but **confirm** via rera.ap.gov.in agent guidelines before onboarding sales teams.

## B) Legal requirements for plot sale

**1. Layout approval** ✅ — **APCRDA** (Amaravati Capital Region) or **DTCP** (rest of AP; dtcp.ap.gov.in, "Layout Search" by LP number); AP Layout Regulations (e.g., G.O. Ms. No. 275 M.A.: min. road width ~40 ft, min. plot ~100 sqm, ~10% open-space reservation — district-specific). ⚠️ Confirm current minimums with DTCP.

**2. Agricultural land conversion** ✅ — AP Agricultural Land Conversion Act 2006: conversion to non-agricultural use; ⚠️ **10% conversion fee on basic land value** commonly cited (verify current %). ✅ Oct 2025 gazette: certain unauthorized layouts validly converted to NA under repealed Act — relevant for legacy ventures. CLU (Change of Land Use) per APCRDA/DTCP for zone changes.

**3. Mutation, encumbrance, title** ✅ — Adangal/land records via Meeseva; **EC from Sub-Registrar**; verify 30-year title chain + no litigation (AP is title-litigation-heavy — include advocate title opinion).

**4. Agreement vs sale deed** ✅ — Same principle as TS: registered agreement ≠ title; **registered sale deed** mandatory; AP courts require full consideration evidence for plot conveyance.

**5. State-specific rules** ✅ — AP RERA Rules 2017; AP Layout Regulations (G.O. 275 M.A. & district GOs); APDPMS (AP Development & Planning Model Services) guidelines; AP Agricultural Land Conversion Act 2006.
