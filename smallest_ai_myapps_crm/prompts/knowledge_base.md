## 1. What a Solar Dealer / EPC / Channel Partner Actually Does in India

### 1.1 Residential Rooftop Solar Dealer

- A dealer buys solar hardware (panels, inverters, mounting structures, cables) from a distributor or manufacturer and sells it to end customers (homeowners, small businesses) or small EPCs. A pure dealer does **not** perform installation with their own team. (Source: Quickestimate, https://quickestimate.co/blog/solar-dealer-vs-distributor-vs-epc, Jul 14, 2026)
- Typical dealer activities: maintain local stock of panels/inverters, generate leads through referrals and local ads, issue quotations and close sales (often via WhatsApp or in person), coordinate with third-party installers or tie-up EPCs for physical work, handle DISCOM-facing net metering paperwork in some cases. (Source: Quickestimate, https://quickestimate.co/blog/solar-dealer-vs-distributor-vs-epc, Jul 14, 2026)
- A 1-3 person operation running a district or city zone. (Source: Quickestimate, https://quickestimate.co/blog/solar-dealer-vs-distributor-vs-epc, Jul 14, 2026)

### 1.2 EPC Partner (Engineering, Procurement, Construction)

- An EPC takes full responsibility from site survey to commissioning: site survey (shadow analysis, roof load, DISCOM feeder mapping), system design, equipment procurement, civil + electrical installation, commissioning, net metering application, PM Surya Ghar subsidy documentation, and AMC post-installation. (Source: Quickestimate, https://quickestimate.co/blog/solar-dealer-vs-distributor-vs-epc, Jul 14, 2026)
- EPC net margins on residential projects: 15â€“22% (gross margins 28â€“35%). Higher margin but also higher risk â€” payment risk, warranty claims, labour cost volatility. (Source: Quickestimate, https://quickestimate.co/blog/solar-dealer-vs-distributor-vs-epc, Jul 14, 2026)
- EPC requires electrical contractor license (state electricity board), MNRE empanelment under National Portal for Rooftop Solar, and GST registration. (Source: Quickestimate, https://quickestimate.co/blog/solar-dealer-vs-distributor-vs-epc, Jul 14, 2026)
- There are 80,000+ registered solar EPCs and installers in India (MNRE, 2025). (Source: Quickestimate citing MNRE, https://quickestimate.co/blog/solar-dealer-vs-distributor-vs-epc, Jul 14, 2026)

### 1.3 Distributor

- A distributor buys large volumes directly from manufacturers (Waaree, Adani, Havells, etc.) and resells to a dealer network across a state or multiple districts. Maintains 500â€“2,000 sq ft warehouse with panel stock in MW, extends credit terms (30â€“60 days) to dealers, provides product training and warranty claim processing. Does **not** interact with end customers. Net margins: 3â€“5%. Working capital need: â‚¹20â€“50 lakh minimum. (Source: Quickestimate, https://quickestimate.co/blog/solar-dealer-vs-distributor-vs-epc, Jul 14, 2026)

### 1.4 Sub-Dealer

- A sub-dealer operates under a dealer's license and purchase agreement without direct manufacturer/distributor relationship. Narrower margins (3â€“5%) but almost no upfront stock investment. Good entry point for someone testing the market. (Source: Quickestimate, https://quickestimate.co/blog/solar-dealer-vs-distributor-vs-epc, Jul 14, 2026)

### 1.5 Channel Partner (Referral / Lead Generation Model)

- Some companies (e.g., Heaven Green Energy, Exolar Energy) offer a pure-referral channel partner model: partners earn commission on each referred project that gets installed, with no upfront investment or franchise fee. Heaven Green Energy: â‚¹15,000â€“â‚¹40,000 per residential installation, â‚¹1L+ per commercial project. (Source: Heaven Green Energy, https://www.heavengreenenergy.com/become-channel-partner)
- Exolar Energy offers a referral commission program with no registration fees, percentage-based commission on completed project value, dashboard to track referrals, and dedicated partnership manager. (Source: Exolar Energy, https://exolarenergy.com/solar-epc-referral-commission-business-associate-income-in-india/, Nov 7, 2025)

### 1.6 Waaree Franchise Partner Scope

- According to Waaree's partner page, franchise partners perform: site survey & product selection, installation (EPC services), operation & maintenance, module cleaning, product sales, tenders & projects, storage and next-gen solutions, and RESCO/OPEX model projects. (Source: Waaree, https://www.waaree.com/partner/)
