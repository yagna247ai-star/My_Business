# Terra Aura - Brand Identity Guide

## 1. Brand Concept
**Terra Aura** (Earthly Glow) is a brand dedicated to the "Eco-Friendly Outdoor Oasis & Wellness" lifestyle. It bridges the gap between natural, sustainable living and high-end wellness experiences.

- **Objective**: To provide eco-conscious homeowners with curated outdoor and wellness products that promote relaxation and grounding.
- **Tone & Vibe**: Relaxing, natural, high-end yet accessible, authentic.

---

## 2. Visual Identity

### 2.1 Logo & Favicon

### 2.2 Color Palette
The "Earthy & Warm" palette is inspired by natural clay, Mediterranean olive groves, and soft twilight skies.

- **Olive**: `#708238` (Primary - Vitality, Growth)
- **Clay**: `#B66A50` (Secondary - Earthiness, Warmth)
- **Mushroom**: `#AD9D91` (Neutral - Calm, Balance)
- **Muted Saffron**: `#DDAA55` (Accent - Sunlight, Energy)
- **Dusty Blue**: `#7E93A7` (Accent - Serenity, Air)

### 2.3 Typography
- **Headings**: *Cormorant Garamond* (Elegant, heritage-inspired serif for a premium feel).
- **Body Text**: *Montserrat* (Clean, modern sans-serif for high readability and accessibility).

## 4. Brand Guidelines
- **Imagery**: Focus on natural lighting (golden hour), authentic textures, and lush greenery. Avoid high-contrast artificial lighting.
- **Whitespace**: Maintain generous whitespace in layouts to evoke a sense of calm and clarity.
- **Copy**: Use a gentle, inviting tone. Focus on benefits like "grounding," "renewal," and "sustainable comfort."
