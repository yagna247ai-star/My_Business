## PART 1: THE HERO AD — "25 సంవత్సరాల నమ్మకం" (25 Years of Trust)

### 1.1 Story Arc (Journey Structure)

```
TRUST (Heritage)  →  SCALE (AP/TS footprint)  →  INNOVATION (EMI)  →  PROOF (RERA/DTCP)  →  INVITATION (Community)
     0–6s                   6–14s                   14–20s                20–26s               26–30s
```

The arc is built on ONE emotional claim: *"25 years ago we promised trust. We kept it. Now we've made land ownership possible for every middle-class family — and we'll keep that promise too."* Every scene exists to make that claim believable.

**Narrator**: Mature Telugu presenter, 50s, silver-grey hair, warm authoritative voice — the face of a 25-year-old company should look like 25 years. He wears a beige/off-white kurta with a subtle shawl, seated in a heritage-styled office (framed site photos, wooden desk, plaque). He is the ONLY on-screen human in clips 1 and 5.

**Visual palette (all clips)**: Warm gold + deep green + off-white. Golden-hour grading. Classic "trusted developer" tones. No cool blues, no harsh studio light.

## PART 3: GOOGLE FLOW PRODUCTION NOTES

### 3.1 Scene Segmentation Strategy

| Clip | Duration | Narration Type | Lip-Sync Needed? | Why Split Here |
|------|----------|----------------|------------------|----------------|
| 1 | 6s | Narrator on camera | **YES** | Opening authority — one sentence, one clip |
| 2 | 8s | VO over montage | NO | Real photos — no sync risk, pure authenticity |
| 3a | 3s | VO over map | NO | Graphic animation |
| 3b | 3s | VO over aerial | NO | AI scene |
| 4 | 6s | VO over family scene | NO | Family doesn't speak — zero sync risk |
| 5 | 4s | Narrator on camera | **YES** | Final trust moment + CTA |

**Rule**: Only clips with the narrator's face need lip-sync. Clips 2, 3, 4 are voiceover-only — this cuts lip-sync failure risk by 60% and keeps Telugu VO quality high. Keep each spoken clip to ONE continuous sentence (max ~8s) — Google Flow lip-sync is most accurate on short, single-sentence takes.

### 3.2 Reference Frames You MUST Prepare (Before Generating)

1. **Narrator character ref** (generate once, use for clips 1 + 5 + Variant C's narrator shot): mature Telugu presenter, 50s, silver hair, beige kurta, heritage office with framed site photos + wooden desk + plaque. Warm golden lighting. ONE image, reused everywhere — consistency is what makes the stitching seamless.
2. **REAL completed-site photos** (user-supplied, clips 2): 3–5 photos of actual completed Abhinandhana sites. These are the credibility anchors — AI cannot invent "25 years of completed sites," and Telugu audiences spot fake montages instantly.
3. **Legacy photo** (if it exists): any 25-year-old project photo or old brochure scan — even one such image massively boosts the heritage claim.
4. **City labels**: Telugu city names as clean text graphics for the map clip (విజయవాడ, గుంటూరు, హైదరాబాద్, విశాఖపట్నం).

### 3.3 Telugu VO + Lip-Sync Workflow

1. **Record Telugu VO FIRST**, per-clip audio files (clip1_vo.m4a, clip2_vo.m4a, ...). Clip durations are tuned to VO pacing, not the other way around.
2. Feed each clip's audio to Google Flow with that clip for lip-sync (clips 1 & 5 only).
3. Stitch in editor: 1 → 2 → 3a → 3b → 4 → 5. Transitions seamless because narrator ref is identical in clips 1 & 5 and montage clips are grade-matched.
4. **Add all on-screen text in the editor** (matches your convention — no burned-in captions in generation). Use a classic Telugu serif for heritage text ("25 సంవత్సరాల నమ్మకం"), bold sans for CTA.
5. Export 720x1280, 24fps. Add a single warm music bed in the editor (soft string/tabla blend, low volume — VO must stay the hero).
6. **Variant A** = clips 1+2+4+5 re-cut to 15s. **Variant C** = separate 5-clip EMI production, same narrator ref.

## PART 4: AD RUNNING STRATEGY

### 4.1 Platform Mix

| Platform | Role | Format | Why |

| **Meta (click-to-WhatsApp)** | PRIMARY — conversion | Facebook Feed + Instagram Reels (9:16) | The entire conversion path runs on WhatsApp (ad → chat flow → community). Click-to-WhatsApp is the ONLY format that drops the lead directly into the chat flow. This is your money platform |

### 4.3 CTA Mechanics — The Handoff to Chat Flow (CRITICAL COORDINATION)

```
Ad (click-to-WhatsApp)
  → wa.me deep link with PRE-FILLED message (chat flow entry keyword):
    https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=హాయ్%20నాకు%20ప్లాట్%20వివరాలు%20కావాలి
  → WhatsApp opens → Business auto-reply (SLA < 60s)
  → Chat flow runs (parallel deliverable) → community invite within first 3 messages
  → Lead joins Abhinandhana WhatsApp Community → warm-up content → site visit → booking
```

**The ad side owns**: the pre-filled text must match the chat flow's first keyword EXACTLY (coordinate with the chat-flow builder — e.g., keyword "ప్లాట్"). The ad's success metric ends at **conversation starts**; everything after is the chat flow + community's job.

**Meta Setup**: Campaign objective = **Conversations** (WhatsApp). Single CTA button: "Send WhatsApp Message". Place the deep link in the ad's CTA destination. Set a WhatsApp Business number on the ad account before launch.

### 4.6 Success Metrics & Decision Rules

| Metric | Target (AP/TS Telugu) | Decision |
|--------|----------------------|----------|
| CTR (link click) | ≥ 2% | Below 1.2% → hook is weak, refresh Variant A/B hooks |
| CPM | ₹150–250 | Above ₹350 → creative fatigue or audience too narrow; refresh or widen |
| CTR-to-WhatsApp | ≥ 1.5% of impressions | Below → CTA mechanics broken (check deep link!) |
| **Cost per WhatsApp conversation start** | ₹80–150 | Above ₹200 → tighten creative; this feeds the chat flow |

**Scaling trigger**: a variant with cost-per-community-join under ₹200 AND CTR above 2% → scale +20% every 48h. **Kill trigger**: no variant under ₹250/join after 7 days → fix the creative (hook), not the budget.

**Frequency cap**: 2–3 impressions/week per user. Refresh creative every 3–4 weeks (new scene edits from the same footage — the montage clips can be re-cut indefinitely).
