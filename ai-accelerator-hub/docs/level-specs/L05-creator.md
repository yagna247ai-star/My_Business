## Shared and protected
**Outcomes.** The learner can:
- produce a coherent content pack across at least 3 media (image, voice or video, presentation)
- follow a repeatable creative workflow
- apply rights, consent and disclosure rules

| # | Activity | Protected rules |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mission Briefing | 1 check question |
| 2 | Learn | 4 lessons: image prompting · video and voice basics · presentation storytelling · responsible content (consent, likeness, copyright, disclosure, deepfakes). Quiz at 60% |
| 3 | Explore GitHub | A verified open-source creative-AI repo from the library: find its licence and its model-use restrictions. 3 questions |
| 4 | Build: Content Pack | Fields:<br>`brief` ≥30 words (audience, goal, message)<br>`assets` (≥3 items: ≥1 image, ≥1 voice-over or short video, 1 slide deck of ≥5 slides)<br>`workflow` ≥50 words<br>`rights` ≥30 words (tool terms checked, no real person's likeness or voice without written consent, source of any music)<br>`disclosure` (the "Made with AI" label used) |
| 5 | Submit Proof | Links to the assets and a screenshot of each tool's terms page as checked |
| 6 | Evaluation and Boss | 6 content situations |

**Boss choices:**
- 0 = OK to publish with an AI label
- 1 = Needs consent or rights first
- 2 = Do not create

## Young Coder
- **Briefing:** The school science fair needs a poster, a 1-minute explainer and slides for their project.
- **Check question:** Is it OK to make an AI voice that sounds like their teacher, for a joke?
  - Yes
  - **No, not without the teacher's permission, and fake voices of real people can hurt them**
  - Yes, if it's funny

  Answer: 1.
- **Learn demo:** Make a poster image with a "no people, cartoon style" prompt, then add their own text.
- **Build:** A science-fair pack: a poster image, a voice-over in their own voice or a generic AI voice, and 5 slides. There are no faces of real classmates.
- **Boss scenarios:**
  1. A cartoon robot for the poster [0]
  2. Using a classmate's photo in an AI video [1]
  3. A fake video of the principal announcing a holiday [2]
  4. Background music from a popular film song [1]
  5. AI slides about their own experiment [0]
  6. A mean meme about a classmate [2]
- **Safety:** Publishing outside school needs guardian consent. No faces, names or uniforms appear.

## Student & Career Starter
- **Briefing:** A college fest needs promotional content, and they want portfolio pieces.
- **Check question:** They used AI images in a portfolio. What should they do?
  - Hide it
  - **Label it as AI-assisted and describe their own creative decisions**
  - Claim they drew it

  Answer: 1.
- **Learn demo:** Storyboard 3 frames, generate them, and keep the style consistent with a reference prompt.
- **Build:** A fest or club promo pack (poster, 30-second video or voice teaser, pitch deck), or a portfolio case-study deck.
- **Boss scenarios:**
  1. AI-illustrated club poster [0]
  2. A celebrity's face in the fest promo [1]
  3. Fake testimonials from "past attendees" [2]
  4. A sponsor's logo in AI art [1]
  5. An AI voice-over in their own cloned voice, with their own consent [0]
  6. An AI image of a rival college's event "disaster" [2]

## Engineering Professional
- **Briefing:** They must present an architecture proposal and a demo video to leadership by Friday.
- **Check question:** AI generated an architecture diagram. Before presenting, they:
  - Present it
  - **Check every component and arrow against the real design, and fix any invented services**
  - Add more colours

  Answer: 1.
- **Learn demo:** Generate a slide outline from an RFC, then cut it to a 5-slide narrative (problem → options → decision → risk → ask).
- **Build:** A technical pitch pack: a deck of ≥5 slides, a narrated demo screencast of ≤2 minutes, and diagram images. A stretch goal is docs-as-code diagrams in the repo.
- **Boss scenarios:**
  1. An AI icon set for internal slides [0]
  2. Using a customer's logo in an external talk [1]
  3. A fabricated benchmark chart [2]
  4. Stock AI art in a conference talk [0]
  5. A colleague's voice for a demo narration [1]
  6. A deepfake of the CEO for an "engagement" video [2]
- **Safety:** Follow employer brand and confidentiality rules. Show no internal data on public slides.

## AI Builder, Enthusiast
- **Briefing:** Their app needs a logo, app-store screenshots, a launch video and a pitch deck.
- **Check question:** Can they use an AI-generated logo commercially?
  - Always
  - **Check the tool's commercial-use terms and do a trademark search first**
  - Never

  Answer: 1.
- **Learn demo:** Make 3 logo variations, then pick one using criteria: readability at 32 px and distinctiveness.
- **Build:** A launch pack: a logo, 3 product images, a 45-second launch video with voice-over, and a 7-slide pitch deck.
- **Boss scenarios:**
  1. AI-generated feature illustrations [0]
  2. A logo similar to a famous brand [1]
  3. Fake user reviews in the launch video [2]
  4. A cloned influencer voice for an ad [1]
  5. AI background music from a tool whose licence allows commercial use [0]
  6. A fake "featured in" press logo [2]

## Business Owner
- **Briefing:** Festival season needs posters, a WhatsApp video and a price-list deck, with no designer budget.
- **Check question:** An AI photo shows food that looks better than the real dish. What should they do?
  - Use it
  - **Use real photos of their actual food, or label it as illustrative, so customers aren't misled**
  - Nothing

  Answer: 1.
- **Learn demo:** Turn their own phone photo of a dish into a clean poster layout, rather than generating fake food.
- **Build:** A festival campaign pack: 2 posters, a 30-second voice-over video in their customers' language, and a 5-slide menu or price deck.
- **Boss scenarios:**
  1. An AI background for a real food photo [0]
  2. A regular customer's photo in an ad [1]
  3. Fake "1,000 happy customers" reviews [2]
  4. A film song for a WhatsApp video [1]
  5. An AI voice-over in their own voice [0]
  6. An AI image implying a hygiene certification they don't have [2]
- **Safety:** Follow advertising-honesty norms, and get customer consent for any photos.
