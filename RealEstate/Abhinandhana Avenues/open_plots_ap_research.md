## 0. Market context: the Amaravati capital factor (applies to all 7 cities)

The single biggest price driver since mid-2024 is the TDP-led government's restart of the **Amaravati greenfield capital** (Vijayawada–Guntur–Guntur-zone of the APCRDA region). Within days of the June 2024 election result, Amaravati plot prices surged **50–100%**, from roughly ₹10,000–15,000/sq yd to ₹40,000–50,000/sq yd (Economic Times, 8 Jun 2024; Business Today, 6 Jun 2024). By 2025–26, capital-region ranges were: **core capital plots ₹45,000–60,000/sq yd; Kuragallu–Nidamarru belt ₹30,000–45,000; Kaza–Namburu ₹24,000–30,000** (market tracker, 2025). The APCRDA region average is ~₹43,834/sq yd (range ₹23,000–72,000) per amaravathipropertyexperts.com. Live 2026 listings: Lingayapalem ₹52,000/sq yd (630 sq yd, toplinerealty.in), Velagapudi ₹45,000/sq yd (600 sq yd), Nidamarru ₹27,000/sq yd (2,000 sq yd). Amaravati-region average rates show ~25.6% YoY growth in early 2026, and a TOI headline (Dec 2025) reports IT-investment push lifting VMRDA plot prices ~60%.

**Spillover effects:** Vijayawada and Guntur are "gateways to Amaravati" and absorbed most of the demand; North Andhra (Vizag, Kakinada, Rajahmundry) and Nellore/Tirupati are seeing steady 6–15%/yr appreciation on infrastructure (IT corridors, airport, ECR) rather than capital speculation.

## 8. Registration values vs market values

- **Charges:** Stamp duty ~5% + registration ~1% in AP (99acres; MagicBricks cites up to 6%/0.1–0.5% — confirm per document).
- **Feb 1, 2025 revision:** AP raised registration/guideline values **15–20%** across districts (Openplot; Deccan Chronicle, 29 Jan 2025) — e.g., Vijayawada Kakani Nagar proposed at ₹45,000/sq yd / ₹3,800/sq ft.
- **Amaravati exemption:** the government **exempted the Amaravati capital region from the revision** to keep registration low and support buyers (Bizz Buzz, 29 Jan 2025) — so market/registration gap there has widened again.
- **Ongoing gap:** CAG audit (2025) and market trackers confirm a "huge gap between guidance value and market rates" in urban AP; per APRMVG Rules values must be revised 1 Aug annually (urban) / alternate years (rural). Typical pattern: registration 40–70% of asking price in growth corridors; nearest parity in Kakinada Ramanayyapeta and Nellore city.

## 9. Authority approvals (APCRDA / VMRDA / TUDA / DTCP) — requirements

- **APCRDA** (capital region, incl. Vijayawada, Guntur, Amaravati): layouts need formal approval with an **LP number**; minimum road width **40 ft**, minimum plot **100 sq m**, **10% open space/community area**; approvals searchable at crda.ap.gov.in Approved Layouts portal (APCRDA Guidelines PDF; AP Land Development (Layout & Sub-division) Rules 2017).
- **VMRDA** (Visakhapatnam, ex-VUDA/VGTM-UDA): VUDA/VMRDA-approved layouts mandatory inside the metro area; verification list published by local portals.
- **TUDA** (Tirupati) and **DTCP** (non-urban-authority towns: Rajahmundry, Kakinada, Nellore) — DTCP-approved layouts (e.g., DBV Raju Villas, Diwancheruvu).
- **RERA:** plotted developments must be RERA-registered in AP; check registration on the AP RERA portal before purchase (99acres guide; eswarigroup 2026 buyer guide).

## 10. RERA-registered plotted projects in AP — count & examples

- **4,000+ projects** registered under AP RERA (as of Feb 2024, 99acres) — the majority are plotted layouts/ventures; plotted-development registrations are rising sharply (215 new plotted projects registered in India in a recent year, +43% YoY — ora.in, 2026).
- Examples: **Peram Group** (91 RERA-approved projects, 25,000+ plots); **BBG True Capital** (VMRD & RERA-approved, LP 09/2022, 570 plots near Amaravati); **BBG @ Kaza** gated community (2025); **Charan Group** Bhogapuram plots (VMRDA & AP RERA); **Eswari Group** (verified 2026 RERA builder); **Capital Royal City** (~70 acres, Kanchikacherla).

## 11. Key takeaways

1. **Amaravati is the price engine**: 50–100% post-election surge (2024), 25.6% YoY (early 2026); the Kaza–Nambur–Gorantla fringe and Vijayawada corridors (Gannavaram, Poranki, Penamaluru) capture spillover at 40–75% of core rates.
2. **Vizag is the only city with 30%+ 3-year plot appreciation** (IT corridors, Bhogapuram airport); North Vizag and Kommadi–Madhurawada are the premium plotted belts.
3. **Kakinada & Nellore offer the best registration-value parity** (guidance ≈ 70–90% of asking), while Amaravati-region registration is artificially low by policy.
4. **Tirupati and Rajahmundry are the value markets** (₹8k–20k/sq yd), with TUDA/RERA-approved layouts abundant; safe entry points for long-hold buyers.
5. **Verify LP number + RERA registration before buying** — unapproved layouts remain the biggest risk in every city (APCRDA min 40-ft roads, 100 sq m plots, 10% open space).
6. All sq-yd figures above are **dated asking/guidance values**; closing prices in corridors typically settle 5–15% below asking.
