## Targeting Recommendations

### Ad Set A — Cold Audience (Broad)
- **Location:** India (tier-1 & tier-2 cities) — or pin specific states
- **Age:** 25-55
- **Gender:** All
- **Detailed Targeting:**
  - Interests: Solar energy, Renewable energy, Solar power, Business owner, Entrepreneurship, Small business
  - OR Behaviors: Small business owners, Business page admins
- **Placements:** Advantage+ (let Meta optimize), but manually exclude Audience Network if quality is low

### Ad Set B — Lookalike
- Upload your existing customer list (emails/phones of past solar buyers)
- Create 1-3% lookalike
- Stack with interests to narrow further if needed

### Ad Set C — Retargeting
- Website visitors (last 30 days)
- Video viewers 50%+ (all your Meta videos)
- Instagram/Facebook page engagers (last 90 days)
- Exclude: People who already submitted the lead form (last 180 days)

## Landing Page / Destination

If NOT using Instant Forms (using website conversion instead):

1. **Remove navigation** — single page, single action
2. **Headline must match ad hook** — don't make them re-learn what you do
3. **Bullet points, not paragraphs** — mobile-first
4. **Calendar embed** — Calendly/TidyCal directly on page
5. **Social proof** — "Trusted by 200+ solar businesses across India"
6. **Urgency** — "Only 10 consultation slots available this week"
7. **Mobile speed** — must load under 3 seconds

---

## What to Track

- CPL by ad set and creative
- Lead quality (call show-up rate, qualification rate)
- Cost per qualified meeting (not just cost per lead!)
- Video metrics: 3-second views, Thumbstop rate, 50% watch, 95% watch
- Drop-off points in the video (Meta shows this in Ads Manager)

If leads are cheap but poor quality → tighten targeting + add a qualifying question in the form.  
If leads are expensive but high quality → scale budget, the unit economics work.
