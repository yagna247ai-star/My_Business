## How to use

1. **Preview:** Open `index.html` → click any card → hit ▶ PLAY. Each is 28-32s, Ken Burns zoom on the blueprint, auto-typed subtitles, black/gold caption bar, yellow CTA pill, pulsing progress bar.
2. **Reaction:** Click `📷 Camera ON` inside any short to enable your live face bubble (top-right). Otherwise duet in Instagram.
3. **Record to MP4:** Mac `Cmd+Shift+5` → select phone frame → record while it plays. Or upload the `.gif` directly — Instagram/YouTube accept GIFs and auto-convert.
4. **Post:** Upload GIF or screen-recorded MP4 as YouTube Short + Instagram Reel + WhatsApp Status.
