# Offer 1 — The ₹5,999 GenAI Strategy Mastermind

## 1. Naming

- **Primary:** "GenAI Command Room" — *For engineers with 10–15 years of experience who refuse to be spectators.*
- Alternatives: "GenAI Strategy Weekend" / "The GenAI Operator Mastermind" / "Decision Weekend"

## 2. Positioning Statement

> This is not a course. It's a decision room. In one weekend, senior engineers walk out with: (1) a personal GenAI strategy, (2) a stack decision they can defend to their CTO, (3) a 90-day rollout plan, and (4) a peer network of leaders facing the same problems.

## 3. Format & Logistics

- **2 days:** Saturday 10:00–18:00 IST, Sunday 10:00–16:00 IST (breaks built in). Run a parallel ET-friendly track for global participants.
- **Platform:** live Zoom + private WhatsApp/Slack cohort.
- **Cohort size:** 30–50 seats (scarcity + intimacy).
- **Replays:** available for 30 days.
- **Price:** ₹5,999 standard · ₹4,999 early-bird (first 20 seats) · ₹4,999 for webinar attendees.
