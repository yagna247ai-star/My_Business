## Shared and protected
**Outcomes.** The learner can:
- run one task across at least 2 assistants and compare them fairly
- build a repeatable research workflow
- fact-check claims against primary sources
- measure time saved

| # | Activity | Protected rules |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mission Briefing | 1 check question |
| 2 | Learn | 4 lessons: choosing an assistant for the job · research with citations · checking claims against sources · a personal productivity system. Quiz at 60% |
| 3 | Explore GitHub | A verified repo's docs on evaluating AI answers, or a model-comparison resource from the library. 3 questions |
| 4 | Build: Assistant Comparison & Research Workflow | Fields:<br>`task` ≥30 words<br>`comparison` (one prompt to ≥2 assistants; a table of accuracy, usefulness and sources)<br>`factcheck` (≥5 claims, each with a primary source URL and a ✓ or ✗)<br>`workflow` ≥60 words (repeatable steps)<br>`timesaved` (minutes before and after, with how they were measured) |
| 5 | Submit Proof | Link plus screenshots of the outputs, with personal data removed |
| 6 | Evaluation and Boss | 6 AI answers; reason ≥6 words |

**Boss choices:**
- 0 = Trust it for this purpose
- 1 = Verify against a primary source first
- 2 = Don't use AI for this

## Young Coder
- **Briefing:** A science project on water pollution needs facts. Two AI tools give different numbers.
- **Check question:** What should they do when two AIs disagree?
  - Pick the one they like
  - **Find the number on an official or educational website and cite it**
  - Average them

  Answer: 1.
- **Learn demo:** Ask two assistants "how many bones in an adult human?" and then a harder question. Check both against a textbook or a reliable site.
- **Build:**
  - Task: a school project topic.
  - Fact-check: 5 claims against school-approved or official sources.
  - Workflow: a study routine (questions → AI explanation → textbook check → own notes).
- **Boss scenarios:**
  1. AI's summary of a story they already read [0]
  2. A historic date for an exam [1]
  3. AI writes their entire essay to submit [2]
  4. A statistic for the project poster [1]
  5. Ideas for a project title [0]
  6. Advice on a medicine dose [2]
- **Safety:** Use only tools allowed for their age and by their school, with guardian awareness. Never sign up with false age details.

## Student & Career Starter
- **Briefing:** Researching a company before an interview, AI gives outdated funding and product facts.
- **Check question:** For "the company's latest product launch", what's safest?
  - Trust the chatbot
  - **Use a cited search assistant and open the company's own newsroom or press page**
  - Skip research

  Answer: 1.
- **Learn demo:** Ask the same company question in 2 assistants, then open the cited links. Some citations don't support the claim.
- **Build:**
  - Task: interview research on 2 target companies, or a literature review for a project.
  - Fact-check: ≥5 claims.
  - Workflow: a reusable "company brief in 30 minutes" template.
- **Boss scenarios:**
  1. Rewording their own notes [0]
  2. The company's revenue figure [1]
  3. AI writes a take-home assignment they submit as their own [2]
  4. Citation details for a paper [1]
  5. Brainstorming interview questions to ask [0]
  6. Answering an online proctored test [2]
- **Safety:** Follow the college's academic-integrity rules. Disclose AI use where the rules require it.

## Engineering Professional
- **Briefing:** An engineer evaluates a library upgrade. AI claims an API exists, but the docs don't show it.
- **Check question:** AI says "use `fooClient.retryAll()`". What next?
  - Ship it
  - **Check the official docs or source for that version and write a small test**
  - Ask the AI again

  Answer: 1.
- **Learn demo:** Ask 2 assistants about a recent library feature, then check the changelog and catch the one hallucinated API.
- **Build:**
  - Task: an engineering research question (library choice, migration path, RFC research).
  - Comparison: across ≥2 assistants.
  - Fact-check: ≥5 technical claims against official docs or source code.
  - Workflow: a "tech spike in 2 hours" routine.
  - Time saved: measured on a real spike.
- **Boss scenarios:**
  1. A summary of a design doc they wrote [0]
  2. A claimed CVE fix version [1]
  3. Pasting proprietary source into an unapproved consumer tool [2]
  4. An API rate limit number [1]
  5. Naming ideas for an internal tool [0]
  6. Legal interpretation of a software licence for the company [2]
- **Safety:** Follow the employer's AI usage policy, and use only approved tools for company code.

## AI Builder, Enthusiast
- **Briefing:** Choosing the stack for an app, different AIs recommend different tools and prices.
- **Check question:** AI says "Tool X has a free tier with 10k users". What should they do?
  - Trust it
  - **Check the tool's current pricing page and note the date**
  - Pick the cheapest

  Answer: 1.
- **Learn demo:** Ask about pricing for 2 no-code tools in 2 assistants, then check the official pricing pages. They see how the AI answers go out of date.
- **Build:**
  - Task: stack or market research for their Level 2 idea.
  - Fact-check: ≥5 claims (prices, limits, competitors) with dated sources.
  - Workflow: a "validate a tool before I build on it" checklist.
- **Boss scenarios:**
  1. Rewriting the app description [0]
  2. A competitor's user count [1]
  3. Copying a competitor's paid template from an AI answer [2]
  4. A tool's API limits [1]
  5. Brainstorming app names [0]
  6. AI deciding the app's privacy policy for them [2]

## Business Owner
- **Why it matters:** They use AI tools every day in their own business. This is an emphasis level for them.
- **Briefing:** They want to know their local food-licence renewal rules. AI gives a confident answer with no source.
- **Check question:**
  - Follow the AI
  - **Get the rule from the official government or authority site, or ask their accountant**
  - Ask a friend

  Answer: 1.
- **Learn demo:** Ask 2 assistants a compliance question, then compare with the official source. Use AI for drafting and the official source for rules.
- **Build:**
  - Task: one weekly business job, such as supplier comparison, a marketing plan or a customer FAQ.
  - Comparison: across ≥2 assistants.
  - Fact-check: ≥5 claims (prices, regulations).
  - Workflow: a Monday-morning AI routine.
  - Time saved: hours saved per week, measured over 1 week.
- **Boss scenarios:**
  1. Drafting a WhatsApp offer message [0]
  2. A GST rate for a new item [1]
  3. Deciding a staff termination [2]
  4. A supplier's claimed price [1]
  5. Ideas for festival combos [0]
  6. Legal advice on a contract dispute [2]
- **Safety:** Don't paste customer lists or bank details into AI tools.
