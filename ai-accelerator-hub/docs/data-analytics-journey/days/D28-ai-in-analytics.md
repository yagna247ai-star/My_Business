# Day 28 — What AI Can and Cannot Do for Your Data Work

> **Time:** 4 h 30 min · **Module:** M6 AI for Data · **Shop:** Sunita Stores

## The problem
Sunita's nephew says: "AI can do your analysis now. Just paste the whole sales file in." Sunita is not sure. Sunita asks three questions:
1. What can AI safely help us with?
2. What can it not do?
3. What must never be shared?

## The big idea
**AI** is a computer that can do tasks that usually need a human to think.

**Generative AI** writes new things: text, code, pictures. A **large language model (LLM)** is the kind of AI that writes text. It works by guessing the most likely next word, again and again.

Because it guesses, it can sound sure when it is wrong. This is called a **hallucination**.

Think of a very fast intern who writes beautifully, but never checks the numbers. You let them draft. You check everything before it goes out.

## Words to know
- **AI**: a computer that does tasks needing thought.
- **LLM**: an AI that writes text by predicting likely words.
- **Hallucination**: a confident answer that is not true.
- **Prompt**: the question or instruction you give an AI.

## Let's solve it
Sort Sunita's weekly tasks into three groups:
- **AI drafts, a person checks**: writing a summary sentence, suggesting a chart, drafting SQL.
- **AI should not do it**: calculating exact totals, deciding a staff member's pay.
- **Only a person does it**: decisions that affect people's money, jobs, or health.

**Never paste these into an AI tool:**
- Customer phone numbers, addresses, or bank details.
- Staff names next to their salaries.
- Health information about a real person.

## Your turn
1. List six data tasks Sunita does in a normal week. Example: a weekly sales summary.
2. For each task, mark one group: AI drafts and a person checks, AI should not, or only a person.
3. For one "AI drafts" task, write the check a person would do.
4. Write three things that must never be pasted into an AI tool.

**Hand in:** a one-page map with the six tasks, the groups, one check, and three never-paste items.

## Check yourself (trainer's answer key)
1. An LLM guesses words, so it can invent a number. Always check numbers.
2. AI is the whole field. Machine learning is one way to build AI that learns from data.
3. A check: run the query, and compare one number by hand.

**Typical answers:** Good "AI drafts" tasks: SQL drafts, summary wording, chart ideas. "Only a person": pay decisions, anything with legal or money impact.

## Why this helps your job
Employers now ask how you use AI tools. A clear answer, with the checks you do, sounds professional.
