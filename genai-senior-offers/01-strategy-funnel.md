# Gen-AI Senior Track — Offer & Funnel Strategy

**Built for:** GEF (GenAI Engineering Fellowship) — repurposing the C6 curriculum for a senior audience (10–15 years of experience)
**Market:** India-first pricing (₹), global delivery (live cohorts, IST + ET tracks)
**Goal:** A multi-rung ticket ladder from a ₹5,999 Mastermind → 3-month senior course → high-ticket coaching → enterprise

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Your unfair advantage:** You already own a battle-tested 24-week curriculum (GEF C6), five named industry mentors (Sidharth, Vishnuvardhan BKM, Ramanathan R, Srishti Gureja, Paras Madan), production-grade capstone projects, and session recordings. Most competitors sell slides; you have a proven delivery pipeline.

**Strategy in one line:**
> *Educate with the free layer → decide in the ₹5,999 Mastermind → build with the 3-month course → scale with 1:1 coaching and enterprise.*

**Core insight:** Senior engineers with 10–15 years of experience do NOT buy "courses." They buy **decisions, leverage, and outcomes** — a stack decision they can defend to their CTO, a 90-day plan, a portfolio system they shipped, and a peer network at their level. Every offer must be framed as: *"Walk in with a problem, walk out with a strategy/system."*

## 2. Audience — The 10–15 Year Professional

**Profile:** Senior Software Engineer / Engineering Manager / Architect / Data Science Lead / Product Lead / small-company CTO.
**Income:** ₹35–90 LPA (India) / $120–220k (global) — **price-sensitive: low; time-sensitive: high.**

**Pain points (ranked):**
1. **Obsolescence fear** — "10 years of experience, but GenAI is changing the rules and juniors know it."
2. **Decision confusion** — build vs buy, RAG vs fine-tune vs agents, which stack to standardize on.
3. **Leadership pressure** — the board/CEO is asking "where is our AI strategy?"
4. **No time** — full-time job + family; cannot sit through a 24-week bootcamp.
5. **Team upskilling problem** — can't hire enough GenAI engineers; must grow their own.
6. **Vendor fatigue** — "everyone is selling AI; I've been burned by snake oil."

**What they will pay for:** clarity, a decision framework, a production reference architecture, same-level peer network, and a "signature project" they can point to.
**What they will NOT pay for:** Python refreshers, toy demos, "AI is amazing" hype.

**Positioning wedge:** *GEF C6 is for builders. This track is for leaders who must decide, direct, and deliver.* Same curriculum core, senior lens.

## 3. Ticket Ladder (Offer Architecture)

| Rung | Offer | Price | Format | Job to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Lead magnet + Webinar | Free | PDF + 90-min live | Capture & warm |
| 1 | **GenAI Strategy Mastermind** | ₹5,999 (launch ₹4,999) | 2-day weekend intensive | Tripwire — convert & qualify |
| 2 | **3-Month GenAI Engineering Leadership Program** | ₹59,999 (launch ₹49,999, EMI 3×₹21,999) | 12-week live cohort | Core product |
| 3 | **Capstone Accelerator / 1:1 Mentorship** | ₹1,49,999 | 12 weeks, 1:1 + production deployment | High ticket |
| 4 | **Enterprise / Team Cohorts** | ₹3L–8L+ per cohort | Custom private cohorts | B2B scaling |
| 5 | Alumni + Certification + Referral | Included / ₹9,999 renewal | Community | LTV & referrals |

**Pricing logic:**
- **10× rule between rungs** (5,999 → ~60K → ~150K) makes upgrades feel like a natural "level up," not a leap.
- ₹5,999 is deliberately below "thinking twice" for this income bracket (≈ one dinner out). The real cost is their weekend — so sell the agenda ROI, not the price.
- **EMI** (3×₹21,999) converts time-poor, budget-conscious buyers.
- **Anchor high:** show the ₹1,49,999 accelerator on the course sales page so ₹59,999 feels safe.

## 4. The Funnel (End-to-End)

### Stage A — Traffic
- **LinkedIn (primary engine):** 3×/week posts — "GenAI decisions for engineering leaders": architecture callouts, build-vs-buy teardowns, GEF capstone highlights. This audience lives here.
- **X/Twitter:** 1×/day threads on agent architecture, evals, RAG gotchas.
- **YouTube:** repurpose 10 GEF sessions into 3–5 min "senior insights" clips featuring the mentors.
- **Communities:** Reddit (r/ExperiencedDevs, r/LocalLLaMA), India AI groups, engineering WhatsApp/Telegram groups — answer, don't pitch.
- **Referrals:** GEF alumni + current cohort introduce senior colleagues (₹2,000 credit per intro).
- **Partnerships:** HR/L&D heads at mid-size product companies, tech conferences.

### Stage B — Lead Magnet (Rung 0)
- **Product:** *"The 2026 GenAI Stack Decision Map for Engineering Leaders"* — a 1-page decision flowchart (RAG vs fine-tune vs agents vs MCP; build vs buy) + 12-page playbook + self-audit checklist.
- **Promise:** *"Decide in 30 minutes what your team would take 6 months to figure out."*
- **Capture:** name, work email, role, years of experience — **use years to qualify the 10–15 bucket** and segment messaging.

### Stage C — Free Webinar ("The GenAI Leverage Session")
- **Title:** *"5 GenAI Architecture Decisions Every Engineering Leader Gets Wrong"* (90 min, live + replay).
- **Structure:** 45 min content (2 decision frameworks from the GEF curriculum) → 15 min live architecture Q&A → 20 min mastermind pitch → 10 min FAQ.
- **CTA:** join the ₹5,999 Mastermind — webinar attendees get ₹1,000 off + seat guarantee.
- **Target: 10–15% of attendees buy the mastermind.**

### Stage D — Mastermind (Rung 1) — the real conversion engine
Full blueprint in `02-mastermind-5999-blueprint.md`. The critical mechanics:
- **Day 1 ends with a live architecture clinic** — solve 2 attendees' real problems on the whiteboard. This is the demo of the course.
- **Day 2 morning:** a 20-min "what you'd build in 12 weeks" session showing the course capstone pipeline (reuse GEF C6 capstone examples: conversational bot, RAG chatbot, AI search engine, multi-agent system).
- **Day 2 end:** early-bird course offer — ₹49,999 instead of ₹59,999 + 6-month EMI, **closes end of the weekend**.
- **Target: 20–30% of mastermind attendees enroll in the course.**

Why this works: the mastermind is a **paid audition**. People who spend ₹5,999 and a weekend with you have self-selected. Selling ₹59,999 to a warm, satisfied room is 10× easier than to a cold list.

### Stage E — Post-Mastermind Nurture (for the 70–80% who don't buy immediately)
- **Day 1:** thank-you + strategy pack + "early-bird ends Sunday" reminder.
- **Day 3:** case study — one senior attendee who applied the framework (or a GEF capstone story).
- **Day 7:** "the cost of waiting" — 3 real examples of teams that made the wrong build-vs-buy call.
- **Day 10:** testimonial + 1:1 call offer ("Let's map your capstone in 15 minutes").
- **Day 14:** final early-bird close → after that, next-cohort waitlist + ₹5,000 future credit.
- **Plus:** a 15-min 1:1 call for every mastermind buyer within 10 days — qualify them for the accelerator (Rung 3).

### Stage F — Course (Rung 2) → Upsell
- **Weeks 8–10:** pitch the Capstone Accelerator (Rung 3) to top performers: *"take your capstone to production with a 1:1 mentor."*
- **End-of-course demo day** (reuse GEF C6 demo-day format) → invite enterprise HR/CTO contacts.

### Stage G — Enterprise (Rung 4)
- **Productize:** *"Private cohort for your engineering org — 12 weeks, 10–25 seats, customized capstones, mentor time."* ₹3L–8L.
- **Trigger:** any senior attendee who asks "can my team join?" → enterprise brief.
- **Outreach:** LinkedIn to CTO/VP Eng, sharing GEF demo-day projects as proof.

### Stage H — Retention & Referral Loop
- Alumni community (Slack/WhatsApp): monthly "architecture clinic" + job/intro referrals.
- Certification renewal ₹9,999/yr.
- Referral: ₹3,000 credit per successful course referral (≈5% CAC rebate).

## 5. Sales Copy Angles (per stage)

**Hero message (mastermind page):**
> *"For engineers with 10–15 years of experience: stop being a spectator to GenAI. Spend one weekend and leave with your strategy, your stack decision, and your 90-day rollout plan."*

**Emotional drivers (weighted for this audience):**
1. Legacy / leadership — "your org expects you to lead this"
2. Fear of being leapfrogged by juniors who know GenAI
3. ROI pragmatism — "one correct architecture decision pays for this 100×"
4. Identity — "leaders decide; learners ask"

**Objection handling table:**

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I don't code much anymore" | The mastermind is decision-first: frameworks, architecture, build-vs-buy — code optional. The course has a leader track where your capstone is directed, not hand-coded. |
| "Too busy" | Two weekends + async labs. Everything recorded. 80% of the value is in the decision frameworks, not seat time. |
| "GenAI is hype" | Five production-grade GEF capstones + mentor war stories from Cohere, Airbus, Target, Athina AI. We show receipts. |
| "₹59,999 is steep" | What does one wrong stack decision cost your org? One correct vendor or architecture call pays for this 100×. EMI available. |
| "Will this be another course?" | You ship a production multi-agent system + an adoption playbook your CTO can sign. That's a portfolio piece, not a certificate. |

## 7. 30-60-90 Day Rollout

**Days 0–30 — Build & test**
- Wk 1: Lock mastermind agenda; record lead magnet; build landing pages (lead magnet + webinar).
- Wk 2: 2 LinkedIn posts/week; run 3 warm webinars (invite GEF alumni + colleagues) — iterate on pitch.
- Wk 3: **Pilot mastermind** (8–15 paid attendees at ₹4,999 launch price) — capture video + testimonials.
- Wk 4: Fix funnel leaks; schedule public Cohort #1 (6 weeks out); open course waitlist.

**Days 31–60 — Launch mastermind publicly**
- LinkedIn/X content engine at full cadence; 2 webinars/week.
- Mastermind Cohort #1 (50-seat cap); pre-sell course early-bird inside.
- Course Cohort #1 (8–15 students); start Week 1.

**Days 61–90 — Scale**
- Course running; pitch accelerator to top performers at Week 8.
- Launch enterprise one-pager; 10 targeted CTO/VP Eng outreaches.
- Review KPIs: double down on the best traffic channel; plan Cohort #2.
