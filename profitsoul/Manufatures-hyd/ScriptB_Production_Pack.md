## 0. Why this pack exists instead of a finished video

**Script B was never recorded.** The only footage supplied is `hyd manufacturers .mp4`,
and it is Script A. Verified, not assumed:

- It opens *"Hyderabad manufacturers — one question"*
  (`ఒక ప్రశ్న`). Script B opens *"one **honest** question"* (`ఒక నిజాయితీ ప్రశ్న`).
- It contains Script A's second paragraph — *"గత నెలలో"* ("last month"), and the
  staff/phone-floor story (`మీ స్టాఫ్ ఫ్లోర్లో ఉంటారు. ఫోన్ మోగుతుంది`). Script B has no
  staff/phone-floor line; its second paragraph is about the last 100 enquiries.
- **None** of Script B's distinctive content appears anywhere in the 76.7 s: no
  "last 100 enquiries", no ₹50,000, no ₹1 lakh, no "handed him to your competitor for free",
  no "you don't need more enquiries".
- A search of the whole workspace found exactly one video file.

Putting Script B subtitles over Script A audio would produce an ad where the viewer
**hears one script and reads another**. That is a broken asset, not a variant, so it was
not built.

Everything else is done — the moment the Script B take exists, the cut is one command
(section 5).

## 3. Shooting brief — please read the framing note

**The one change that materially improves the ad:** in Script A, Yagna's mouth sat at
**52% of frame height** and his chin at **60%**. Meta's 9:16 safe zone reserves the
bottom 35%, so legible captions could not fit inside the safe band without covering his
mouth. I kept the mouth clear and anchored captions at 70% — a deliberate compromise.

**Fix it at the shoot, not in the edit:**
- Frame with **minimal headroom** — top of head ~8–10% from the top of frame
- That puts the chin at roughly **50%**, which frees the whole 52–65% band for captions
  inside the strict safe zone

Unchanged from Script A (the PDF calls for continuity):
- Blazer/coat, clean background, strong lighting **on the face**
- Direct to camera, same confident energy and tone as Script A
- Record portrait 9:16. **Set the camera higher and closer** — if you shoot this on a
  phone, use the main (wide) lens, not digital zoom
- Record in a quiet room; the Script A take had **audio clipping at 0.0 dBFS** — leave
  headroom this time (peaks around −6 dBFS)

**To build:** drop the new take in as `hyd manufacturers B.mp4`, record its cue timings in

```bash
cd work

python3 assemble.py upload
```

Outputs are 2160×3840 and 1080×1920, H.264 High, 30 fps, loudness −14 LUFS / −1.0 dBTP —

**language auto-detect** (not `language="te"`) returns usable timing anchors. See

## 6. Alternative if you cannot reshoot

If a second ad is needed before a re-shoot is possible, the honest option is a **variant
cut of Script A** — e.g. tighter framing on the face, pauses trimmed to ~55 s, and
captions repositioned. That gives you two creatives to split-test, which is genuinely
useful for Meta delivery.

It is **not** Script B, and it should not be labelled as such: the retargeting logic
depends on Script B's distinct opening (money already lost), which only exists in the
Script B performance. Say the word and I'll build it.
