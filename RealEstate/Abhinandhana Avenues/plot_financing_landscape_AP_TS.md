# Financing / EMI Landscape for Open Plot Purchases in India — Telangana & Andhra Pradesh

**Prepared:** 5 Aug 2026 · **Scope:** Hyderabad/Vijayawada plotted-development market · **Method:** web research (rates/terms cited with URL + date; estimates clearly flagged)

---

## A) Builder-Financed EMI Schemes (MOST RELEVANT)

### How developers structure them
Open-plot developers in Hyderabad/Vijayawada overwhelmingly use **in-house EMI schemes** rather than bank-financed purchases. The dominant structures observed in live listings:

| Structure | Typical terms (from listings) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed down payment + fixed EMI | ₹50k–₹4L booking + ₹10k–₹25k/month | Koravi Developers: ₹16.8L plot, ₹4L booking, ₹10k/month EMI (Instagram, Jan 2026) |
| % booking + balance in EMIs | 20–50% upfront, balance in 12–36 monthly EMIs | A.M Infra: 40–50% initial + 18-month interest-free EMIs (Instagram, 2026) |
| Pure zero-interest EMI | 24-month, 0% interest, price per sq.yd | Maaz City Kothur: ₹15,000/sq.yd, 24-month EMI, 0% interest (Instagram, 2026); A1 Township: 24 months @ 0% |
| Zero-down schemes | 0 down + EMI (high risk, rare) | Elite Venture "zero down payment" (Facebook listing) |
| "No-cost EMI" | 0% markup marketed; usually price-loaded | Common across Hyderabad listings (24acres.in, 2026) |

**Most common in Hyderabad/Vijayawada:** booking 10–30% (₹80k–₹4L), balance over **24 months** (12–36 range), monthly EMI ₹8k–₹25k, **registration deferred until ~50–100% of dues are paid**. Examples: DTCP plots at ₹80k booking + ₹14,500/month EMI for 150 sq.yds (24acres.in, 2026); Skandhanshi Infra (AP, KUDA-approved): ₹25k/month, 30-month flexible tenure (Facebook, 2026).

### Legal structure & risks
- **Structure:** a *sale agreement + payment schedule* (not a loan). Title transfers by registered sale deed only at the end; buyer holds agreement rights meanwhile. Some developers execute a **general power of attorney / irrevocable GPA** or keep the land as collateral till full payment.
- **Buyer risks (confirmed pattern):** forfeiture clauses for missed EMIs (10–20% of paid amount often non-refundable); developer financial failure leaves buyer with only an agreement; "no-cost" schemes often carry a **loaded base price** (typically +5–10% vs. cash price); title/approval issues (DTCP/HMDA/KUDA vs. unapproved layouts — buyers must verify).
- **Seller/developer risks:** default after possession without deed → repossession is legally messy; double-sale risk if agreement not registered; no recourse if buyer abandons mid-way other than forfeiture.
- **Market terms to know:** "flexi payment", "24 months EMI", "no-cost EMI plots", "0% interest", "pre-EMI", "construction-linked" (CLP) vs "time-linked" (TLP) — flexi = hybrid of down payment + time-linked installments (99acres.com "flexi-payment plan"; Adani Realty payment-plans blog, Aug 2025).

### Pros / Cons
- **Pros:** no bank eligibility/credit-score gate; 0% or low effective cost vs. bank interest; fast, developer-managed; flexible for self-employed cash-income buyers common in AP/TS.
- **Cons:** no RERA-style protection for pure plot agreements in many cases; delayed registration (buyer unprotected in insolvency); price loading; penalty clauses; no tax benefits (unlike home loans under §24/§80C for construction).

## B) Bank Loans on Plots (Land Purchase Loans)

**Yes — banks lend on open residential plots, but terms are stricter than home loans.** Confirmed current (2025–26) numbers:

| Lender | Rate (p.a.) | LTV | Tenure | Source (date) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HDFC Bank Plot Loan | 7.75%–13.20% (Repo 5.20% + 2.50–7.95%) | Up to 80% (≤₹75L); 75% above; **restricted to 70% outside city limits**; direct-allotment only | Up to 15 yrs | HDFC plot-loan page (fetched 5 Aug 2026) |
| SBI | from ~7.25% (promo) onward; ~9% typical 2025–26 | up to 70% of plot value | up to 10–15 yrs | Paisabazaar SBI plot loan; SBI Realty blog (Apr 2026); YouTube comparison (2026) |
| ICICI Bank | ~8.50–9.70% (home-loan slab); land loan product live | — | up to 15–20 yrs | ICICI land-loan page (Aug 2026) |
| LIC Housing | — | max 75% of property value | max 15 yrs | LIC Housing plot loan page (2026) |
| Market range (all banks) | ~9.40–9.85% salaried; ~10.90% self-employed | 50–60% (MagicBricks) to 80% (Paisabazaar); 65–75% (Tata Capital) | 10–15 yrs vs 30 for home loans | MagicBricks (30 Jan 2026); Paisabazaar (2026); Tata Capital (Jan 2026) |

**Key rules (confirmed):**
- **LTV is lower than home loans** — commonly 50–75% (vs. 80–90% for homes); the rest is down payment (PNB Housing blog, Aug 2026: 20–40% down payment, CIBIL 650+).
- **Tenor capped** at 10–15 years (vs. 30 for home loans); rates run **~0.5–1% higher** than home loans (SBI ~9% vs HDFC ~9.2% for 2025/26 — YouTube comparison; Tata Capital blog, 8 Jan 2026).
- **Restrictions:** banks finance plots in approved (DTCP/HMDA/VUDA/KUDA) layouts; agriculture/panchayat land generally excluded; **no construction top-up** — plot loan and later construction loan are separate applications; some lenders require construction to start within a defined period (typically a few years) or convert to a construction loan; funding outside city limits often capped at 70% (HDFC, confirmed).
- **Banks active in AP/TS:** SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Bank of Baroda, Canara, Union + HFCs (LIC Housing, PNB Housing) all operate plot-loan desks in Hyderabad/Vijayawada; processing fee ~0.35–0.50% (salaried) at SBI/HDFC.
- **No tax benefit** on interest for plot-only loans (benefit starts once construction loan is taken/construction begins).

## D) Chit Funds & Informal Finance (South-India Specific)

- **Chit funds** (registered under Chit Funds Act, Telangana/AP have large registered chit sectors) are a traditional AP/TS route: members pool monthly contributions, winners take a lump sum (often used for plot bookings), others treat it as forced saving. **Common for booking money rather than full plot finance.** *Estimate, not confirmed via dated source — verify current chit company rules with state registrars.*
- **Gold loans** (banks + NBFCs like Muthoot/Manappuram, 9–25% p.a.) are the most-used informal bridge for land purchases in semi-urban AP/TS; **loan-against-agricultural-land** products (Bank of Baroda, Bank of India, Canara) finance farm/agri land purchases (confirmed — BoB agri-land scheme; Bajaj "loan against agricultural land" article notes informal lenders charge **exploitative rates** in rural areas, pushing borrowers to informal credit).
- **Implication for developers:** many plot buyers in tier-2 AP/TS fund the 20–30% booking via chit/gold, then developer EMIs — i.e., informal finance **complements** builder EMI schemes rather than replacing them.

## E) NRI Financing

- **FEMA rules (confirmed, multiple sources 2025–26):** NRIs/PIOs/OCIs may buy **residential and commercial** property (incl. residential open plots) freely; **agricultural land, plantation property and farmhouses are prohibited** (MEA/FEMA FAQ; Omaxe blog Aug 2024; assetlyhq Mar 2026). Open *residential* plots in DTCP-approved layouts are permissible.
- **Repatriation:** sale proceeds are repatriable if the property was bought with funds remitted from abroad (NRE/FCNR) — up to **USD 1 million/financial year** from NRO balances; properties acquired under §6(5) FEMA need RBI approval to repatriate (RBI FAQ; assetlyhq Mar 2026). TDS 1% + 15CA/15CB filing on remittances.
- **NRI loans:** HDFC/ICICI/SBI offer NRI home/plot loans — NRI processing fees are higher (HDFC: up to 1.50% vs 0.50% resident — confirmed on HDFC page), and LTV for NRI applicants is typically lower. NRI demand is significant in Hyderabad (largest NRI diaspora city) and Amaravati corridor.

## F) Government Schemes

- **PMAY-U (confirmed):** **subsidy is NOT provided for purchasing land/plot alone** — "subsidy will not be provided for purchasing a land"; a loan for *land + construction* qualifies only if capped at **₹25 lakh** combined (IDBI PMAY 2.0 FAQ PDF). PMAY funds house purchase/construction, not plots.
- **Telangana Housing Board:** e-auctions of developed plots + LIG/MIG flat lotteries; LIG income band ₹25k–50k/month (eauctionsindia blog, Aug 2026); Swagruha scheme gives ~₹5L assistance for house-building on own land (99acres TS Housing Board article, 2026). Plot allotments are lottery/auction-based — not a general EMI route.
- **Andhra Pradesh:** APCRDA runs Amaravati land allotment under the **Amaravati Land Allotment Regulations 2017**; plot allotments to farmers via e-lottery (The Hindu, 23 Jan 2026); LPS (Land Pooling Scheme) plots are tradeable. No state-wide "EMI plot" subsidy scheme; benefits are project-specific (e.g., CRDA LPS incentives).
- **Bottom line:** no govt scheme subsidizes open-plot purchase for private buyers — builder EMI + bank/NBFC loans remain the only practical routes.

---

## Bottom-Line for Plot Developers' Customers (Hyderabad/Vijayawada)

1. **Builder EMI is the default market route** — typically 20–30% booking + 24-month (12–36) zero/low-interest EMIs, registration at the end. Verify DTCP/HMDA/KUDA approval, check if price is loaded, and get the agreement registered/notarized.
2. **Bank plot loans are cheaper where eligible** (~7.25–9.9% vs. 0% but price-loaded builder plans), but need 25–50% down payment, approved-layout plots, 10–15-yr tenors, and no construction top-up.
3. **NBFCs fill the credit-score/self-employed gap at 9–14%.**
4. **NRIs can buy residential plots (not agri land)** and repatriate sale proceeds up to USD 1M/yr with proper sourcing.
5. **Govt schemes do not fund private plot purchases (PMAY confirmed excluded);** chit funds/gold loans are the common informal complement for booking money in AP/TS.
