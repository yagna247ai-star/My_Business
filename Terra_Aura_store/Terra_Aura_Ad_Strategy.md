# Terra Aura — Meta Ad Creative Strategy & Launch Sequence

## Product: Terra Aura Cordless Portable Blender
- **Retail Price**: Sub-\$120
- **Brand Positioning**: Eco-Wellness. Grounding, renewal, sustainable comfort.
- **Color Palette**: Olive `#708238` / Clay `#B66A50` / Mushroom `#AD9D91` / Muted Saffron `#DDAA55` / Dusty Blue `#7E93A7`
- **Typography**: Headings — Cormorant Garamond (serif, elegant). Body — Montserrat (sans-serif, clean).

## PART 3: Recommended Ad Formats by Market

### US Market

| Format | Priority | Rationale |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Video (15–30s)** | Primary | Best for demonstrating the blender in action — the quiet blend, the portability, the aesthetic. Meta's CBO heavily favors video. Match the brand's cinematic, golden-hour aesthetic. |
| **Carousel** | Secondary | Showcase 3 color variants (Olive, Clay, Dusty Blue) in lifestyle scenes. Each card = one use case (morning smoothie, gym, office, outdoor). Good for retargeting. |
| **Static Image** | Testing Only | Single hero image + testimonial quote overlay. Lower priority — the product's key selling points (quiet blend, portability) are better shown than told. |

### India / Telugu Market

| Format | Priority | Rationale |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Video (15–30s)** | Primary | Video consumption on Instagram/Facebook Reels in India is massive. Telugu voiceovers with emotional hooks perform best. Use the Reels-native 9:16 format. |
| **Carousel** | Secondary | Use for the "Ayurveda + Technology" angle — each card shows a traditional ingredient transforming into a modern smoothie. Also effective for "before/after" comparison (Angle 4). |
| **Static Image + Text Overlay** | Testing | In the Indian market, information-dense static ads (Telugu text, specs, price) can outperform video for certain audiences, especially on Facebook feed. Test against video for value-conscious segments. |

**Format Note**: Both markets — always produce in **9:16 vertical (1080x1920)** for Reels/Stories placement AND a **1:1 square** version for Feed. One shoot, two crops.

### Phase 1 Success Metrics & Decision Rules

| Metric | Target (US) | Target (India) | Decision at Day 14 |
|--------|------------|----------------|---------------------|
| **CTR (Link)** | > 1.2% | > 1.5% | Below 0.8% → kill ad set |
| **CPC** | < \$1.20 | < ₹15 | Above target → pause, analyze creative |
| **Add to Cart Rate** | > 4% | > 5% | Below → landing page or price objection check |
| **ROAS (7-day click)** | > 1.0x | > 1.5x | Below → do NOT scale; fix conversion path first |
| **CPM** | < \$25 | < ₹200 | Above → audience saturation or poor engagement signal |

**Winning Angle Definition**: Lowest CPA (cost per purchase) at the highest spend that maintains ROAS above target. If 2 angles tie → run Phase 2 with both.

---

### Phase 2: Scaling the Winner (Days 15–30)
**Objective**: Scale winning angles profitably. Test secondary formats.

| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Scale Rule** | Increase winning ad set budget by 20% every 48 hours as long as ROAS holds. Never more than 30% in one jump (triggers re-learning). |
| **Format Expansion** | Take the winning angle's hook and body copy, produce a **Carousel** version for retargeting. |
| **Retargeting** | Launch 1 retargeting ad set per market: 30-day website visitors, video viewers (75%+ watch), Add to Cart abandoners. Budget: 15% of prospecting spend. |
| **Lookalikes** | If 100+ purchases, build 1% LAL from purchasers. Budget: \$15/day US, ₹400/day India to test. |

---

### Phase 3: Profit Maximization (Days 31–60)
**Objective**: Expand to new audiences while defending ROAS.

| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Interest Testing** | Test 3–5 interest-based audiences (e.g., "Smoothies," "Yoga," "Sustainable living" — US; "Health food," "Organic products," "Cooking" — India). Max \$10/day each. |
| **Creative Refresh** | Produce 1 new video per winning angle to combat ad fatigue. Same angle, different execution (new location, new talent). |
| **Advantage+ Shopping Campaign** | If 50+ purchases in 30 days, launch ASC with all winning creatives. Let Meta optimize across placements automatically. |

## PART 6: Production Notes for AI Video Workflow

Based on your existing workflow with Omni/Clip prompts and Telugu voiceover production:

1. **Voiceovers**: Record Telugu VO first — use the script from Angle 3. Then sync AI video cuts to the audio pacing. The Telugu VO timing will determine shot lengths, not the other way around.

2. **Key Frame Generation Order**:
   - Generate still key frames first using your image model
